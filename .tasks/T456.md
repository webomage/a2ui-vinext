# T456 — Unified Headroom service management via Homebrew services and just helpers

## Summary

Following the rebase of `webomage/headroom` (`feat/omlx-proxy-routing`) onto upstream v0.39.0 and resolution of proxy streaming/cache defects, unify Headroom proxy execution across the estate. Instead of running two competing unmanaged LaunchAgents (`com.headroom.default` on port 8787 and `sh.brew.headroom-local` on port 8788), manage a single unified multi-upstream Headroom proxy via Homebrew services (`casualuser/tap/headroom`) on port 8787. Provide canonical estate `just` recipes (`just headroom-status`, `just headroom-start`, `just headroom-stop`, `just headroom-restart`, `just headroom-logs`) via `project/scripts/headroom_service.py` following the established `omlx_service.py` pattern ([[T374]]).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC1 — Author `wom7web/project/scripts/headroom_service.py` to manage Headroom service lifecycle (status, start, stop, restart, logs, health probing on port 8787).
- [ ] AC2 — Wire canonical `just` recipes in estate root `justfile` and `wom7web/justfile` (`headroom-status`, `headroom-start`, `headroom-stop`, `headroom-restart`, `headroom-logs`).
- [ ] AC3 — Upgrade `casualuser/tap/headroom` Homebrew formula to 0.39.0-w0m (pointing to `webomage/headroom` branch `feat/omlx-proxy-routing` with `HEADROOM_NO_CCR=1`).
- [ ] AC4 — Decommission and unload conflicting legacy LaunchAgents (`com.headroom.default` and `sh.brew.headroom-local`), unifying execution on port 8787 and freeing port 8788.
- [ ] AC5 — Author unit tests in `wom7web/project/scripts/tests/test_headroom_service.py` and verify all quality gates pass organically.
