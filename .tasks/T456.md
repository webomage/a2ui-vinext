# T456 — Unified Headroom service management via Homebrew services and just helpers

## Summary

Following the rebase of `webomage/headroom` (`feat/omlx-proxy-routing`) onto upstream v0.39.0 and resolution of proxy streaming/cache defects, unify Headroom proxy execution across the estate. Instead of running two competing unmanaged LaunchAgents (`com.headroom.default` on port 8787 and `sh.brew.headroom-local` on port 8788), manage a single unified multi-upstream Headroom proxy via Homebrew services (`casualuser/tap/headroom`) on port 8787. Provide canonical estate `just` recipes (`just headroom-status`, `just headroom-start`, `just headroom-stop`, `just headroom-restart`, `just headroom-logs`) via `project/scripts/headroom_service.py` following the established `omlx_service.py` pattern ([[T374]]).
