# T602 — Enforce TECH-DEBT.md Tracked grammar (D54), reconcile 16 paid debt rows, and harvest session location/parameter friction on worktree landing

## Summary

Auditing why `TECH-DEBT.md` was neither consistently read in the right location nor updated when tickets closed exposed three coupled gaps:
1. **Path & instruction drift:** `.agents/rules/sdlc.md` still pointed to pre-`T312` paths (`w0m-web/TASKS.md`, `project/tickets/T##.md`), and `w0m-sdlc-archive/SKILL.md` instructed agents to *"DELETE rows it paid down"* without mentioning the `T331` `## Closed-Debt Ledger` contract or `just debt-check --enforce`. Meanwhile, agents frequently guessed wrong paths (`w0m-web/TECH-DEBT.md`, `w0m-web/justfile`, `w0m-web/project/scripts/mcp_ratchet.py`, `w0m-web/project/deps-policy.json`, `scripts/agent_tree.py`) because scripts and policies span root `scripts/`/`project/policies/` and `w0m-web/project/scripts/`.
2. **Ungated `Tracked` column (`D54`):** Because `TECH-DEBT.md`'s `Tracked` column had no grammar separating active ticket ownership (`owner:`) from discovery provenance (`found-at:`) or accepted risk/ratchets (`accepted:`), `scripts/debt_register_check.py` treated all **28 rows referencing `done` tickets** as informational (`[INFO] UNOWNED`) and exited `0` under `--enforce`. In reality, **12 of the 40 active rows** (`D3`, `D4`, `D10`, `D11`, `D13`, `D17`, `D21`, `D34`, `D43`, `D45`, `D49`, `D64`) were already 100% paid down in code, and **4 more** (`D26`, `D46`, `D54`, `D56`) are small hygiene fixes we paid down directly.
3. **Uncollected session friction at worktree landing:** When an agent hits file-location misses (`FileNotFoundError` / `file does not exist`), CLI parameter errors, or gate failures during a worktree session, those errors sat in session transcripts (`transcript.jsonl` / `.claude/projects/*/*.jsonl`) and were never harvested at `just agent-tree-land` time to feed `LESSONS.md`, `TECH-DEBT.md`, or follow-up tickets.

## Acceptance criteria

Promoted `stale_tracking` (`STALE OWNER`) from informational to gating (`--enforce` exits `1`) when an `owner:` (or bare `T##`) row references only `done`/`archived` tickets, while keeping `found-at:` and `accepted:` rows valid when their referenced tickets exist. Proven RED and GREEN in `scripts/tests/test_debt_register_check.py`.
- [x] **AC2 — Reconcile `TECH-DEBT.md` against live reality and pay down `D26`, `D46`, `D54`, `D56`**: Moved the 12 already-paid debt rows (`D3`, `D4`, `D10`, `D11`, `D13`, `D17`, `D21`, `D34`, `D43`, `D45`, `D49`, `D64`) plus the 4 newly-paid rows (`D26`, `D46`, `D54`, `D56`) into `## Closed-Debt Ledger` with verified provenance (`24` active rows + `44` closed-ledger rows, `0` findings); set `mcp: null` when `probed: false` in `scripts/mcp_ratchet.py` + `w0m-web/project/mcp-exposure-baseline.json` (`D26`); added `"packageManager": "pnpm@10.32.0"` to `w0m-ext/package.json` (`D46`); and skipped comment lines (`#`, `//`, `--`, `*`) in `w0m-web/project/scripts/version_doctor.py` (`D56`) with red/green unit tests (`25/25` pass).
