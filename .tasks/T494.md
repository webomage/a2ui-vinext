# T494 — Public personal and company profiles

## Summary

Split from [[T439]] (CP IA). Menu slot and page route reserved during T439 planning.
Builds public-facing view of member and company profiles:
- Portfolio showcases and project highlights.
- Confirmed test credentials and skill badges.
- Verified connected accounts and badges.
- Privacy controls defining which fields are public vs member-only.
