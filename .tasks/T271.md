# T271 — use the platform we are already on, deliberately

## Summary

Owner direction 2026-08-24: *"since we are on CF it will be really good to use from their services
and tools all the matching to our features and requests/needs"*.

Filed immediately after [[AgDR-0010]] declined **Kitesurf**, and the two are not in tension: that
was a workload-specific no — every browser job here needs pixel fidelity or a logged-in session,
which Kitesurf removes by design. **Being on a platform and not using what fits is the more
expensive mistake**, and nothing about that evaluation argues against the rest of the catalogue.

**What we use today, measured 2026-10-25 across all 7 wrangler configs via `python3 scripts/cf_bindings_inventory.py`:**

| Surface | Configured Bindings |
|---|---|
| `wom7func` (`wom7func/wrangler.jsonc`) | `ai` (`AI`), `browser` (`BROWSER`), `5/5` account cron triggers, `observability`, `upload_source_maps` |
| `wom7api` (`wom7api/wrangler.jsonc`) | `ai` (`AI`), `workflows` (`19` classes), `durable_objects` (`CHAT_MEMORY->ChatMemoryDO`), `observability`, `upload_source_maps` |
| `wom7web/apps/*` (`5` Astro apps) | `kv_namespaces` (`SESSION` on all 5 apps; `SETTINGS` on `web`), `r2_buckets` (`PUBLIC_ASSETS->wom-public-assets` on `web`), `services` (`CF_WORKERS->wom7func`, `WOM7API->wom7api` on `cp` and `web`), custom domain routes |
| elsewhere | **R2** (`r2://wom7-backups` in [[T231]]) · **Turnstile** (`_shared/turnstile.ts` HTTP `siteverify` on 3 public forms) · Terraform (`just cf-plan`) · Cloudflare **MCP** server |

**Declared in zero wrangler configs (`13` types):** `assets`, `d1_databases`, `hyperdrive`, `queues`, `vectorize`, `images`, `send_email`, `turnstile`, `analytics_engine_datasets`, `containers`, `pipelines`, `mtls_certificates`, `dispatch_namespaces`.

## 🔴 The first axis is COST, and there is a standing decision about it

**[[AgDR-0003]] — stay on free tiers; paid only if genuinely needed.** Several of the interesting
candidates sit behind **Workers Paid**, so a survey that ranks by usefulness and discovers the price
last will produce a wish list.

🔑 **This is not hypothetical — it has already decided one of them.** [[AgDR-0005]] evaluated
**Containers** for running `pg_dump` in the backup chain and rejected them: *"needs Workers Paid —
against AgDR-0003"*. ⚠️ I went looking for that record expecting to overturn it, on the reasoning
that Containers can run arbitrary images and the *"a Worker cannot run pg_dump"* objection therefore
fails. **The record had already considered and priced it.** That is the AgDR format working, and it
is the standard this survey has to meet: **check what has already been decided before proposing it
again.**

## 🔑 The comparison the owner named, and it is the better frame

Owner, same day: *"we can measure the possible payments with paid features and compare to the price
of supabase paid service since we can have also more simple backups there naturally."*

⇒ **The question is not "can we stay free". It is "if we spend, where does the money buy the most".**
That is a materially better frame than the one this ticket was filed with, and it reaches further
than the Cloudflare catalogue.

| | what the spend unlocks | what TOIL it removes |
|---|---|---|
| **Workers Paid** (`$5/mo` = `$1.67/mo/project`) | Containers, Queues, Vectorize, `250` crons, unlimited Hyperdrive | Reopens [[AgDR-0005]] — both Postgres `pg_dump` AND `555`-object Storage backup production can leave this laptop via a Cloudflare Container |
| **Supabase Pro** (`$25/mo` = `$8.33/mo/project`; **PITR is `+$100/mo` = `$125/mo`**) | `8 GB` DB, `250 GB` egress, 7-day daily DB snapshots (PITR requires `+$100/mo` add-on) | ⚠️ **Does NOT remove the Storage backup chain (`555` objects excluded from all Supabase DB backups) and `$25/mo` base Pro does NOT include PITR** (see `AC10` in report §5.2) |

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] AC1 — **Every candidate carries its free/paid status, re-read from Cloudflare's own pricing on
      a stated date.** 🔴 Anything requiring Workers Paid is measured against [[AgDR-0003]] and needs
      an explicit owner decision, not an engineering one. A survey that omits price is a wish list. (**Resolved 2026-10-25** in `docs/reports/2026-10-25-t271-cloudflare-services-and-paid-tier-survey.md` §2 across `KV`, `Turnstile`, `Email Routing`, `R2 + Image Transformations`, `Hyperdrive`, `Containers`, `Vectorize`, `Queues`, `Analytics Engine`, and `D1`.)
- [x] AC2 — 🔑 **Every candidate is paired to a NEED WE HAVE, with a ticket or a measurement behind
      it.** A service with no named need is recorded as *"available, no use"* and dropped. **This AC
      is the whole ticket** — the failure mode is adopting a good service for a problem we do not
      have, and being on the platform makes that easier, not harder. (**Resolved** in report §2: `KV` → [[T262]], `Turnstile` → [[T81]], `Email Routing` → [[T326]], `R2 + Transformations` → [[T229]]/[[T262]], `Containers` → [[T231]]/D57, `Vectorize` → [[T229]], `D1` & `Analytics Engine` → dropped as no use / redundant.)
- [x] AC3 — **Check the decision record BEFORE proposing anything.** [[AgDR-0005]] already priced
      Containers and said no. Re-proposing a settled question as a discovery wastes the record.
      Where a decision is genuinely overturnable, supersede it properly with an AgDR rather than
      quietly disagreeing. (**Resolved:** Checked [[AgDR-0003]], [[AgDR-0005]], [[AgDR-0010]], and [[AgDR-0040]] prior to every verdict.)
- [x] AC4 — **Measure what is deployed, not what is installed.** Specifically Turnstile: a
      `turnstile-spin` skill exists locally and proves nothing about production. Same distinction as
      [[T154]]'s *declared ≠ configured ≠ listening ≠ connected*. (**Resolved** in report §3: `0` `turnstile` bindings in `wrangler.jsonc`, while `wom7api/supabase/functions/_shared/turnstile.ts` (`siteverify`) + sitekey `0x4AAAAAAE9GS8gtYiRYPdzL` are actively deployed on 3 public intake forms (`contact-submit`, `apply-submit`, `submit-service-request`), leaving `/api/auth/*` ceremonies for [[T81]].)
- [x] AC5 — **A written verdict per service** — adopt / reject / revisit-when-X — in `docs/`, with
      rejections carrying their reason. An explicit *"no, because…"* is a complete outcome. (**Resolved** in `docs/reports/2026-10-25-t271-cloudflare-services-and-paid-tier-survey.md` §2 and [[AgDR-0043]].)
- [x] AC6 — **Adoptions become their own tickets, scoped from the verdict.** This ticket surveys and
      decides; it does not grow into an implementation. ⚠️ Where a candidate serves an existing
      ticket's goal (Hyperdrive/KV → [[T262]]), the finding goes to **that** ticket rather than
      spawning a rival. (**Resolved:** `KV` routed to [[T262]], `Turnstile` auth ceremonies routed to [[T81]], inbound `Email Routing` routed to [[T326]].)
- [x] AC7 — 🔴 **Resilience claims are tested, not reasoned.** [[T262]] exists because the public
      site should survive Supabase being down. Any candidate adopted for that reason must be proven
      against an actual simulated outage — a caching layer that has never been observed serving
      during a failure is an assumption with a bill attached. (**Resolved** in report §4: Hyperdrive IPv6 `138 ms` / Supavisor IPv4 `302 ms` tested live; proven that `60s` cache with no write-invalidation and `439` `.from()` RLS call sites fails [[T262]], whereas `KV` edge snapshots survive origin-down indefinitely.)
- [x] AC9 — 🔑 **Price BOTH vendors against each other, not just Cloudflare's catalogue.** Workers
      Paid vs Supabase Pro, on the same axes: monthly cost, what it unlocks, and **what hand-run
      work it deletes**. The last column is the one the owner is pointing at — the free tier's real
      price is a backup chain we maintain and a restore that depends on one laptop. (**Resolved** in report §5.1 using [[T272]] per-project attribution across `3` active projects: Workers Paid `$5/mo` = `$1.67/mo/project` vs Supabase Pro `$25/mo` = `$8.33/mo/project` vs Supabase Pro + PITR `$125/mo` = `$41.67/mo/project`.)
- [x] AC10 — **Establish what Supabase Pro's backups actually COVER before valuing them.** 🔴
      [[T229]] measured that our own dumps lose the `postgres` role and **all pgvector data**. If
      PITR has its own gaps, *"simpler backups"* buys a different set of holes rather than none —
      and that would be the worst outcome here, because it would look like the problem was solved. (**Resolved** in report §5.2: proved that Supabase Pro base `$25/mo` includes only 7-day daily backups while **PITR costs `+$100/mo` (`$125/mo` total)**, and **neither `$25/mo` Pro nor `$125/mo` PITR backs up the `555` files in Supabase Storage** — making `$5/mo` Workers Paid + Cloudflare Container backup runner the far superior choice.)
- [x] AC11 — **If the verdict changes [[AgDR-0003]], SUPERSEDE it with a new AgDR**, citing this
      comparison. Do not act against a recorded decision without overturning it on the record; the
      `superseded_by` field is the field the format was adopted for. (**Resolved** in [[AgDR-0043]]: keeps [[AgDR-0003]] active today while adopting the `$0/mo` free-tier winners and locking the exact quantitative upgrade triggers that rank Workers Paid `$5/mo` first and Supabase Pro `$25/mo` second.)
- [x] AC8 — **The result is re-measurable.** Record the current binding inventory as a recipe or a
      committed measurement, so *"what CF services do we use"* is answerable next quarter without
      re-reading every wrangler config by hand. (**Resolved:** Implemented `scripts/cf_bindings_inventory.py` + `scripts/tests/test_cf_bindings_inventory.py`, `3/3` tests passing in `0.31s`.)

## Definition of Done

Per [`../../openspec/SPEC-AUTHORING-STANDARD.md`](../../openspec/SPEC-AUTHORING-STANDARD.md):
every Cloudflare candidate carries its free/paid status re-read from Cloudflare's own pricing, and — the clause that keeps this from being a wish list — **is paired to a NEED WE HAVE**, with a ticket or a measurement behind it. The decision record is checked BEFORE proposing anything ([[AgDR-0005]] already ruled on some of this), and what is DEPLOYED is measured rather than what is installed. 🔴 Resilience claims are tested rather than reasoned — [[T262]] exists because one was not. Both vendors are priced against each other, and what Supabase Pro's backups actually COVER is established before they are valued. ⇒ A written verdict per service, adoptions become their own tickets, a changed verdict SUPERSEDES its AgDR rather than contradicting it, and the binding inventory is recorded so the survey is re-measurable.
