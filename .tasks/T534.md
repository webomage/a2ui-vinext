# T534 — Blueprint provisioning in engagement setup

## Summary

Owner, 2026-10-01: blueprints are in general managed by the admin only; provisioning a blueprint, with selected technologies and the tech chips activated, belongs in the flow of engagement setup. Research it, then spec it. This replaces the idea (T525) of moving blueprints into Me: a Me view of "blueprints I authored" cannot exist because the table has no author column.

**Measured 2026-10-01 (read-only), what exists:**
- `public.blueprints`: 27 rows (24 `public`, 3 `internal`); write access is the capability `blueprint.publish` (policy "Blueprint writers hold the capability"), read is public. The nav entry stays `kept-in-org`.
- `public.blueprint_technologies` (blueprint, technology, role, component, notes, sort order): **0 rows**. No blueprint is linked to any of the 80 technologies, so there is nothing to provision from yet.
- `public.projects`: 7 rows, 1 with a `blueprint_id`, **0** with `metadata.tech_chips`.
- Tech chips live in code: `apps/cp/src/lib/project-archetypes.ts` (archetypes with `default_tech_chips`), used by `ProjectManager.svelte` (`customTechChips`, written to `project.metadata.tech_chips`). Not a table; not tied to blueprints.
- Engagements are the [[T458]] model (S6, the engagement backfill, is paused behind [[T468]]); project and hat setup is [[T461]].

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] AC1 — **Research recorded.** How blueprints, technologies, archetypes and tech chips relate today and where they should meet in setup: which of `blueprint_technologies`, archetype defaults and `project.metadata.tech_chips` is the single source for "technologies in this engagement" (one home for the fact), and what the engagement-setup flow in [[T458]] and [[T461]] looks like at the step where a blueprint is chosen. Findings in `.agents/reports/`, with a recommendation per open question.
- [x] AC2 — **Spec.** An OpenSpec change: choose a blueprint at engagement setup; its technologies are listed with their roles; the admin (or the PM, if the policy allows) ticks which tech chips to activate; provisioning writes the engagement's technologies once and records which blueprint and version they came from. The change cites `design-system/` and `@webomage/ui` for the setup step.
- [x] AC3 — **Blueprint management stays admin-only.** A probe proves a non-admin can neither create nor edit a blueprint nor its technology links, and that the read stays public for `public` blueprints and hidden for `internal` ones.
- [x] AC4 — **Data.** The 27 blueprints get their technology links by a reviewed migration (the owner approves the mapping; no guessing), and the single source for engagement technologies is migrated, with the old `metadata.tech_chips` path removed in the same change (clean cutover, no shim).
- [x] AC5 — **Build.** The setup step, with tests first and mutation proof, and a Gauge spec for the flow.
