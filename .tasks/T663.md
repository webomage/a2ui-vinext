# T663 — Streamline public Audit and Blueprint Order flows to URL-only -> Register and move verification, setup, and results into guided Client Portal

## Summary

Redesign the public **Security & Performance Audit** (`/audit/`) and **Blueprint Order** (`/blueprints/order`) experiences alongside the authenticated **Client Portal** (`apps/cp`) setup and workbench flows so public pages act as frictionless conversion gates and all technical setup lives guided inside Client CP:

1. **Public Website (`/audit/` & `/blueprints/order`) — URL-Only Step 1 -> Register Step 2 -> Portal Handoff**:
   - Currently, `/audit/` stacks four heavy page bands, two competing tab bars inside `SecurityAuditWidget.svelte`, a second always-open form inside `AuditPricing.svelte`, `ComplianceAuditCatalogExplorer` at the bottom, and dumps raw DNS TXT / `.well-known/w0m-verify.txt` instructions right on the public page after order creation.
   - Blueprint pages (`/blueprints/[...slug].astro`) bury `Deploy This Blueprint & Onboard →` inside the off-screen `#spec-drawer` and redirect to `/audit/?target=blueprint&blueprint=<slug>`, losing blueprint card context, variant selection, and monthly cloud cost visibility.
   - Both public flows must lead with a **single URL input field** (plus visual blueprint summary card & scale variant selector on `/blueprints/order`), advance inline to **Register** (work email, lazy Turnstile, `@webomage/fingerprint`, ToS consent -> `POST /api/security-audit` -> `submit-service-request` -> `client-register` magic-link provisioning), and hand off directly to `cp.webomage.com` without exposing DNS TXT / `.well-known` setup walls or `@k7*` jargon on the public marketing surface.
2. **`/services` Relocation**:
   - Move `ComplianceAuditCatalogExplorer` (`client:visible`) from `/audit/` to `/services` (`ServicesPage.astro` / `ComplianceBox.astro`) with a direct CTA into Client Portal onboarding.
3. **Guided Step-by-Step Verification, Scope Configuration, & Results Inside Client Portal (`apps/cp`)**:
   - Upgrade `ClientDashboard.svelte` (`o/[org]/projects`) with a guided 3-step setup banner/checklist for pending domain verifications and queued blueprint/cloud orders plus a streamlined `@webomage/ui`-conformant service launcher.
   - Upgrade `SecurityAuditReportViewer.svelte` (`o/[org]/projects/security-audit`) with a clear 3-step guided progress header (`1. Confirm Domain Ownership` -> `2. Run Perimeter & Lighthouse Scans` -> `3. Remediate & Scale Tiers 2–4`) and view filter tabs (`Overview & All`, `Domain & Perimeter`, `Lighthouse & CWV`, `Code & Cloud (@k7*)`, `Compliance Catalog`) so clients complete verification, configure blueprint/infra details, and inspect unlocked reports inside CP without a 1,600-line vertical wall.
