# T605 — Add Simroute-grade smart visitor fingerprinting (@webomage/fingerprint) and canonical email normalization to prevent multi-trial and free-audit farming

## Summary

Split from [[T604]] to enforce single-responsibility ticket hygiene:
- Build Simroute-reverse-engineered smart visitor fingerprinting in `@webomage/fingerprint` (`w0m-web/packages/fingerprint/src/index.ts`) and `@webomage/lib/security/passive-audit.ts`.
- Compute deterministic `sfp_<sha256>` visitor fingerprints from stable hardware/rendering vectors (WebGL unmasked vendor/renderer `37445`/`37446`, screen geometry, CPU/memory buckets, timezone/locale consistency) plus automation/headless telemetry (`navigator.webdriver`, `SwiftShader`, `llvmpipe`, `HeadlessChrome`, missing `window.chrome.runtime`) and a `0.0–1.0` trust score.
- Normalize canonical emails (`normalizeCanonicalEmail`) by stripping `+tag` suffixes, collapsing Gmail/Googlemail dot-aliases, and rejecting known disposable email providers.
- Enforce `evaluateTrialAndAuditAbuseGuard` across `/api/security-audit`, `/api/client/security-audit`, and `w0m-api/supabase/functions/client-register/index.ts` so a visitor cannot farm multiple free audits or register duplicate trial accounts.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] **AC1 — Smart visitor fingerprint & trust score (`@webomage/fingerprint`)**: Implement `collectSmartVisitorSignals` and `computeSmartVisitorFingerprint` in `w0m-web/packages/fingerprint/src/index.ts` producing `sfp_<sha256>`, `signalStabilityHash`, `trustScore`, and headless/automation risk flags.
- [x] **AC2 — Canonical email normalization & trial/audit abuse guard (`@webomage/fingerprint`, `@webomage/lib/security/passive-audit.ts`)**: Implement `normalizeCanonicalEmail` (`+tag` stripping, Gmail dot-alias normalization, disposable domain detection) and `evaluateTrialAndAuditAbuseGuard` blocking repeat free audits or trial signups matching `visitorFingerprint`, `deviceId` (`w0m_did`), or `canonicalEmail`.
- [x] **AC3 — API & registration enforcement (`/api/security-audit`, `/api/client/security-audit`, `client-register/index.ts`)**: Wire fingerprint and canonical-email abuse checks into `apps/web/src/pages/api/security-audit.ts`, `apps/cp/src/pages/api/client/security-audit.ts`, and `w0m-api/supabase/functions/client-register/index.ts`, with unit test coverage in `packages/fingerprint/src/__tests__/fingerprint.test.ts`.
