## Summary

Split from [[T478]] (owner, 2026-09-29: close T478 at "environment fixed, failures triaged"; leftover scope becomes
its own ticket). T478 run 4 (150 specs, local stack, guard on): 291/442 scenarios passed, 151 failed, 34 min.

## Measured (T478 run 4)

35 golden scenarios in 3 specs (`web/goldens`, `visual/web_visual`, `visual/join_visual`) fail with ~66k-112k mismatched
pixels per page; `job/goldens` and `join/goldens` fail with "No golden for desktop-<name>" (absent baselines).
`web/goldens` alone spends 140 s on 30 mismatches. Goldens captured on live or another machine will differ from a
local render by fonts, data and GPU, so the comparison measures the environment, not the page.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC1 — **Decide the golden source of truth:** rendered on the local stack with seeded data (regenerated by one
  recipe), or on a pinned render environment; recorded as an AgDR.

  closed, as today.
- [ ] AC3 — **A change to seed data or fonts cannot silently pass**: the recipe that regenerates goldens prints the
  diff summary and requires the change to be committed.
- [ ] AC4 — **Local full run: 0 golden failures on an unchanged tree**, measured twice.
