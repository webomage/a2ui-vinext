# T783 — Author Mexico and Global SaaS Business Automation Research Dossier and AgDR-0059

## Summary

Delivers a source-grounded research dossier and architectural decision (`AgDR-0059`) covering the legal, tax, fiscal, payment, FX, privacy, consumer-protection, and AI governance mechanics required to operate Webomage as an automated SaaS & engineering services business from Mexico (Playa del Carmen, Quintana Roo) serving domestic Mexican and global B2B/B2C customers:

1. **Mexican Fiscal & Legal Entity Strategy:** Persona Física RESICO (`626`) vs. Actividad Profesional (`612`) vs. S.A.S. / S. de R.L. de C.V. vs. dual-entity structure, including the RESICO shareholder incompatibility constraint and 3.5M MXN annual cap.
2. **SAT CFDI 4.0 & 0% vs. 16% IVA Export Rules (LIVA Art. 29 Fracc. IV):** Foreign export trace (`XEXX010101000`, `UsoCFDI: S01`, `ObjetoImp: 02`, `IVA 0.000000`), SPEI intermediary settlement risk, and PAC API selection (`Facturapi` vs. `Finkok` vs. `SW Sapien`).
3. **Payment Stack Decision Matrix:** Direct Stripe + `tax_id_collection` / Stripe Tax + Facturapi + Mercado Pago/SPEI vs. full Merchant of Record (`Paddle` / `Polar` / `Lemon Squeezy`).
4. **Regulatory Compliance:** Post-INAI LFPDPPP reform (DOF March 20, 2025 -> **Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno (SABG)**), PROFECO LFPC 5-day renewal notice & Click-to-Cancel / RPCA scope, Banxico SIE FIX FX automation (`mercadopago-checkout`), chargeback evidence automation, and AI governance / E&O thresholds.
