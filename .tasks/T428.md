the **hand-written** `⭐ PICK UP HERE — LATEST` section (`STATE.md` ~113-154). Every session rewrites it,
so two parallel sessions collide there, and T425's additions-only register rule will stop such lands.

Proposal:
- each session writes its handoff into its own ticket's `## Status notes` (disjoint per ticket);
- "PICK UP HERE" becomes a generated section built from the most recent notes;
- this changes the `w0m-state-save` and `w0m-state-load` skills (canonical in wom7skills).
