# T691 — Equal-Height Cards and Bottom-Aligned CTA Links Across Public & CP Grids

## Summary

Enforce equal card heights (`auto-rows-fr items-stretch`, `h-full flex flex-col justify-between`) and bottom-aligned CTA links/buttons (`mt-auto pt-4`) across all card grids on `/solutions/`, `/services/`, `/projects/`, `/blueprints/`, and `/audit/` across mobile, tablet, and desktop breakpoints.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] AC1 — **Solutions, Services & Projects Grids (`SolutionsGrid.astro`, `ServicesDetailed.astro`, `ProjectsGrid.astro`):** Added `auto-rows-fr items-stretch` to the grid containers, `h-full flex flex-col justify-between` to each card wrapper, `flex-1` to body copy, and `mt-auto pt-2` to bottom CTA links across `apps/web`, `apps/cp`, and `packages/ui`.
- [x] AC2 — **Blueprints Gallery & BlueprintCard (`BlueprintGallery.astro`, `BlueprintCard.svelte`):** Added `auto-rows-fr items-stretch` to `BlueprintGallery.astro` and `h-full flex flex-col justify-between` + `mt-auto` footer pinning in `BlueprintCard.svelte`.
- [x] AC3 — **Audit Pricing & Compliance Catalog Cards (`AuditPricing.svelte`, `ComplianceAuditCatalogExplorer.svelte`):** Added `auto-rows-fr items-stretch` to tier/package grids and pinned all `Commission Tier` / `Order Readiness Packet` action buttons to the bottom line via `mt-auto`.

## Definition of Done

Per [`w0m-web/openspec/SPEC-AUTHORING-STANDARD.md`](../w0m-web/openspec/SPEC-AUTHORING-STANDARD.md):
- Acceptance criteria AC1–AC3 verified via `w0m-web/apps/web/src/tests/audit-blueprint-order-ui.test.ts` and `w0m-web/apps/web/src/tests/lighthouse-funnel-hygiene.test.ts`.
- Quality gates clean (`just dod-check`, `just state-check`).
