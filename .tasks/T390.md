# T390 — Surface informative warnings, advisories, and ratchets in Pre-Push Final Report

## Summary

The w0m pre-push hook ([`.husky/pre-push`](file:///Users/proto/Projects/@self/@wom/wom7web/.husky/pre-push)) executes 20 gates generating hundreds of lines of terminal output. Critical informative warnings, advisories, ratchets, and capacity notices (such as Astro check unused variables, w0m audit reputation staleness, dependency skews, CLI tool updates, unowned tech debt rows, STATE.md byte budget warnings, and DoD ratchet improvement notifications) scroll past and are buried, leaving only a single final `✅ [wom7web] Pre-push checks passed.` line.

This ticket delivers `project/scripts/push_report.py` integrated into the pre-push runner and logging wrapper ([`scripts/hook_log_wrap.sh`](file:///Users/proto/Projects/@self/@wom/wom7web/scripts/hook_log_wrap.sh) / [`.husky/pre-push`](file:///Users/proto/Projects/@self/@wom/wom7web/.husky/pre-push)). It parses the run output log, aggregates findings by category, and prints a structured, high-visibility **Pre-Push Final Report** directly before exit on both success and failure runs.
