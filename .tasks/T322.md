# T322 — Bootstrap wom7clips — SMB short-form video content subproject

## Summary

New w0m subproject: generates short thematic videos ("shorts"/"reels") for small-medium
business marketing content, publishing to Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts. Reuses
architecture and SOTA-model research already done for the sibling project personaje0
(`/Users/proto/Projects/_self/personaje0`) rather than re-deriving it — verified present in
that codebase before this ticket was written: the provider-abstraction interfaces
`IVoiceEngine`/`IVisualMotionEngine`/`ILLMEngine` (`src/dbos/interfaces/provider_contracts.py`,
tested), the `HardwareProfile` detector (`src/dbos/runtime/hardware_detector.py`, tested), the
vault pattern (`src/dbos/vault/passport_vault.py` + `asset_vault.py`, adapted here to a **Brand
Kit** identity — logo/palette/voice/guidelines — instead of a persona/passport), the Wan2.2
mlx-video / Kokoro-82M / CosyVoice / LatentSync model choices from
`docs/RESEARCH_REFRESH_AUG_2026_WAN22_MLX_OMLX_ZEROCLAW.md`, and personaje0's still-unbuilt
Omnichannel Publishing Queue + Phase-5 multi-tenant SaaS design (TenantWorkspace/RBAC/
white-label, per its ROADMAP.md) as the template for onboarding multiple SMB clients.

No real-person impersonation concerns — this is business marketing content, not a synthetic
persona/companion product, so personaje0's identity-consent/KYC guardrails don't apply here.

This ticket covers the BOOTSTRAP only: standing up the repo and porting the reusable
architecture. It does not cover shipping a first working end-to-end video — that is follow-on
work once the scaffold exists.
