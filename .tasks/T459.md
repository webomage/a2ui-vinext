# T459 — Research: merge a personal org into a company org

## Summary

Owner, 2026-09-26 (T458 Q32): a person's public profile is separate from their personal org, and a personal org that
is a party to any engagement is **kept**, so completed engagements are preserved. As an **option**, a person who also
belongs to a company could merge their personal org into that company, transferring all its engagements. The owner
asked for this to be researched in detail later, including the commercial side and everything connected to an
engagement (contracts from Upwork and other sources, invoices, repositories, secrets, projects, portfolio items).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] AC1 — Inventory everything that references an org through an engagement (contracts, invoices, projects, repos,
  secrets, vaults, portfolio items, hats) and what each transfer means commercially (a synced Upwork contract names a
  person, not a company; invoices carry legal parties).
  - **Evidence:** Completed 12-surface relational, commercial, fiscal, and RLS inventory across `public.orgs`, `public.org_units`, `public.engagements` (`ON DELETE RESTRICT`), `upwork.*` + `public.source_links` (`AgDR-0029`), `public.contracts` (`T101`), `public.invoices` + Stripe/Mercado Pago/Banxico SPEI (`T351`), `public.projects` (`projects_host_name_lower_uq`, `T204`) + `public.project_repos` (`T287`), `public.project_vaults` + `public.vault_notes` (`T284`), `public.connections` + `public.oauth_states` (`T442`), `public.projects.published_case_slug` (`T288`) + `public.profile_experience`, `public.hats` + `public.hat_assignment_log` (`T461`), and `public.outreach_clips`/`outreach_threads`/`outreach_messages` (`AgDR-0033`, `T561`) + `public.client_message_threads` (`idx_client_message_threads_client_org`, `T285`). Documented in `.agents/reports/2026-10-25-t459-personal-to-company-org-merge-research.md` §2 (mirrored in `wom7web/docs/reports/2026-10-25-t459-personal-to-company-org-merge-research.md`) and `docs/decisions/AgDR-0048-personal-to-company-org-engagement-attribution-and-merge-policy.md`.
- [x] AC2 — Decide per item: transfer, copy, or keep on the personal org; state what can never move (issued invoices,
  third-party platform records).
  - **Evidence:** Authoritative 15-row disposition matrix in `.agents/reports/2026-10-25-t459-personal-to-company-org-merge-research.md` §3 and `AgDR-0048` §Decision.1 classifying every entity into: (1) **Never Move (Immutable on Personal Org / Profile)** — `orgs` (`kind = 'personal'` when referenced by any engagement), completed `engagements` and closed `contracts`/`client_milestones`/`engagement_closures`, `upwork.*` mirror tables and `source_links`, issued/paid `invoices` and Stripe/SPEI/Mercado Pago records, `hat_assignment_log`, and `profile_experience`/`profile_cv_facts`/`profile_resumes`; (2) **Copy / Cross-Link (Dual Portfolio Attribution)** — project case studies (`published_case_slug`, `T288`) attributed to the founder's company org with explicit provenance, and `outreach_clips`/`upwork_evidence_passages` shared via `org_id`; (3) **Transferable (Active / Unbilled / Prospective Only, via Cutoff Novation or Pipeline Reassignment)** — prospective `outreach_threads`, `leads`, and `DRAFT` contracts (direct `org_id` reassignment), and in-flight commercial engagements via **Cutoff Novation** (closing the personal engagement at the cutoff date and opening a successor engagement on the company org for active `projects`, `project_repos`, and `project_vaults`).
- [x] AC3 — Recommend whether to build the merge, with a weighted decision; if built, it is its own ticket.
  - **Evidence:** Evaluated 3 architectural options across 5 weighted criteria in `.agents/reports/2026-10-25-t459-personal-to-company-org-merge-research.md` §4 and locked in `AgDR-0048`: Option 2 (**Non-Destructive Attribution & Cutoff Novation Model**, score **4.85 / 5.00**) defeats Option 1 (Destructive In-Place `org_id` Merge, score 2.05 / 5.00) and Option 3 (Status Quo Manual Recreation, score 3.20 / 5.00). Destructive in-place org merge is permanently declined (`AgDR-0048`), closing the destructive merge option with its legal, fiscal, and relational rationale per Definition of Done.
