# T461 — Hat management in the CP

## Summary

Owner, 2026-09-26, settling Q5 of [[T458]]: the PM of an engagement assigns its hats, including `tl` and the hats under
it, and to themselves; the root assigns anyone anywhere; custom hats and mixes are crafted in the CP; hat collections
are chosen at project creation, each with fallback rules (default: staffing a collapsed role retracts the lead's
fallback, as when the PM takes the `dev` hat). Today only an admin can touch roles or hats. Decision: [[AgDR-0028]]
decision 12. Spec: OpenSpec change `2026-09-26-hat-management`.

## Definition of Done

T461 is done when a PM wearing `pm` can staff their engagement and compose hats in the CP, the root can do it anywhere,
a project starts from a collection, the fallback rule behaves as specified, every refusal is proved by a red probe, and
the specs are archived.
