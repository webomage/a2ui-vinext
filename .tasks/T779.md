# T779 — Replace hardcoded role/currency social hiring presets with selectable Role, Currency, and Timezone filters

## Summary

On `https://me.webomage.com/jobs` (and `apps/cp` Outreach Hub / `/me/connectors`), `SocialHiringSearch.svelte` still exposes two hardcoded combined preset buttons (`DevOps / SRE (USD · EST)` and `Full-Stack (CAD · Americas)`) plus click-to-cycle `Currency` and `TZ` buttons instead of standard selectable filters.

Replace the hardcoded preset buttons and cycler buttons with `@webomage/ui` `Select` dropdown filters (`Role`, `Currency`, `Timezone`) while preserving neutral initial defaults, `Fully Remote Only`, `Save as My Default` / `Reset Default`, platform/discovery switches, `Quick stacks`, and `ds-conformance` baselines (zero native `<select>` elements, zero hardcoded hex colors).
