# T716 — Audit and Fix AgDR-0054 Mobile/Tablet Portrait & Landscape Orientation UX Across All w0m-web Apps (web, cp, me, demo) & @webomage/ui

## Summary

Audit and remediate multi-orientation layout, viewport containment, horizontal overflow, sidebar persistence, and coarse-pointer touch-target regressions across all four `w0m-web` applications (`apps/web`, `apps/cp`, `apps/me`, `apps/demo`), `@webomage/ui` (`packages/ui`), and `@webomage/lib` (`D5_responsive_4_posture` in `packages/lib/src/security/advanced-audit.ts`) against the 4-Posture Mobile-First Multi-Orientation Responsive Contract (`AgDR-0054`: Mobile Portrait `375×812`, Mobile Landscape `812×375`, Tablet Portrait `768×1024`, Tablet Landscape `1024×768`, and Desktop Well-Wrapped `1440×900`).

*(Note: Originally requested alongside `[[T706]]` under provisional label `#707`; filed as `T716` because `[[T707]]` was concurrently allocated on `origin/beta` to `job-hunter-skill`.)*

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] AC1 — **`@webomage/ui` Shell, Header, Overlay & Touch-Target Multi-Orientation Hardening (`packages/ui`):** Fix `AppShell.svelte` (`768×1024` Tablet Portrait `localStorage` persistence & orientation-rotation wipe, `812×375` short-height landscape drawer classification + `48px` compact header + `env(safe-area-inset-*)`, `minmax(0, 1fr)` grid tracks, and `1440px` `--cp-wrapper-max-width`), make `SharedHeader.astro` (`apps/me` & `apps/demo`) mobile-responsive without horizontal overflow at `375px`, bound `dialog-content.svelte`, `QuickViewModal.svelte`, `BugReportWidget.svelte`, and `ContextSwitcher.svelte` within `100dvh` with scroll on `812×375` landscape, set `viewport-fit=cover` in `BaseLayout.astro`/`BaseHead.astro`, and enforce `>= 44px` coarse-pointer tap targets (`button.svelte`, `SideNav.svelte`, `PageHeader.svelte`).
- [x] AC2 — **`apps/web` Mobile/Tablet Portrait & Landscape Fixes (`Header.astro`, `Footer.astro`, `ChatApp.svelte`, `ServiceActionOrderConfigurator.svelte`, `AuditPricing.svelte`, Grids):** Fix `Header.astro` mobile/landscape drawer (`100dvh` + `overflow-y-auto` + compact padding on `max-height: 600px` so `#nav-free-audit-cta` is reachable on `812×375`), bound `ChatApp.svelte` (`.chat-popup` & `.maximized`) within `100dvh` across `812×375` and `1024×768`, eliminate `whitespace-nowrap` button overflow in `ServiceActionOrderConfigurator.svelte` 2-col card grids, fix `Footer.astro` `375px` social row wrapping, and align `AuditPricing.svelte` / `SolutionsGrid.astro` / `BlueprintGallery.astro` to 2-column Tablet Portrait (`768×1024`).
- [x] AC3 — **`apps/cp`, `apps/me` & `apps/demo` Multi-Orientation Fixes:** Fix `ClientDashboard.svelte` (`flex-wrap` header, `cp-onboarding-service-wizard` button wrapping & `auto-rows-fr`, `sm:grid-cols-2 xl:grid-cols-4` stat grids, `client-order-works-panel`), `SecurityAuditReportViewer.svelte` (responsive `.is-screen-preview` dossier grids & tablet 2-col stat grids), `SubscriptionManager.svelte`, `SidebarHatSwitcher.svelte` (`812×375` popover viewport clamp), `AIAgentWorkspace.svelte` & `admin/tasks.astro` mobile/landscape stacking, `apps/me/src/pages/assessments/[id].astro` (`flex-col lg:flex-row` + `100dvh`), `JobBoard.svelte` & `InterviewSession.svelte`, and `apps/demo/src/pages/index.astro` (`minmax(min(100%, 300px), 1fr)` + `app="demo"` + token purity).
- [x] AC4 — **`D5_responsive_4_posture` Static Audit Accuracy, Regression Tests & Multi-App Audit Report:** Fix `D5_responsive_4_posture` (`(?<!max-)(?<!min-)\bw-\[`) and `D4_card_grid_rhythm` accuracy in `packages/lib/src/security/advanced-audit.ts`, align `VIEWPORTS` (`375×812` & `812×375`) + horizontal overflow assertions in `tests/steps/ui.steps.ts`, add Vitest unit/orientation regression tests (`AppShell.test.ts`, `advanced-audit.test.ts`), update `AgDR-0054` app list (`apps/{web,cp,me,demo}`), and publish `.agents/reports/t716-mobile-tablet-orientation-audit.md` (mirrored to `w0m-web/docs/reports/`).
