# T186 — re-merge wom7md into wom7web, then decommission wom7md

## Summary

Owner decision, 2026-08-08, reversing the 2026-07-22 split: *"I was in mind to move docs and
analysis to separate wom7md repo but now I see that tickets and specs still will be in wom7web — so
better to remigrate everything from wom7md and deprecate/delete it."*

🔑 **The reversal is justified by a measurement, not a preference: the split never completed, and
the half that moved is the half that is cited.** All **four** design documents in `wom7md/40-specs/`
are referenced from `wom7web` — **14 inbound references** — while the tickets, specs, ROADMAP rows
and TASKS rows that cite them never left. [[T117]]'s design lives in one repo and its ticket,
OpenSpec change and board row live in another.

⚠️ **Record why the ORIGINAL reason no longer holds, or the next agent re-splits it.** `CLAUDE.md`
states *"Design docs are moving OUT to the `wom7md` vault"*, and the reason was Obsidian. **That
reason is void:** `wom7web` is **already an Obsidian vault** — `.obsidian/` exists in both repos and
**two MCP servers are registered**, `obsidian-wom7md` *and* `obsidian-wom7web`. Consolidating does
not cost Obsidian; it removes a second vault.
