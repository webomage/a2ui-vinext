## Why it exists

*"What are the top 10 hot?"* was answered by an agent re-deriving it from prose every session. Two
agents got two answers, and a hand-written list is stale the moment a ticket moves. Ranking from
**recorded fields only** makes the answer reproducible and, more importantly, **arguable** — the
score is a visible number with a visible breakdown (`just tasks hot --parts`), so disagreeing with it
means disagreeing with a field, which is a fixable thing.

⚠️ **It is a heuristic, not judgement**, and its own footer says so: a ticket blocked on an owner
action scores LOW even when it matters enormously, because the score measures what can be *worked*.
`just tasks blocked` is the surface for those.
