# Universal Calendars & Schedule Integration in Me Next to Todo

## Summary

Add full multi-provider Calendar & Schedule integration in `me.webomage.com` (`/me/calendars`) directly next to **To-dos & wishlists** (`/me/todos`) under `Personal Hub`:
1. **All Calendar Providers & Protocols (`@webomage/lib/calendars`)**:
   - Support Google Calendar, Microsoft Outlook / Microsoft 365 / Exchange, Apple iCloud Calendar, Generic CalDAV (Fastmail, Nextcloud, Radicale, Baïkal, Zimbra), Proton Calendar, Notion Calendar / Cron, Calendly, Cal.com, and Universal `.ics` / `webcal://` subscriptions + `.ics` file import/export.
2. **Bidirectional To-do ↔ Calendar Integration**:
   - Every Todo item in `public.todo_items` with a `due_at` timestamp renders automatically on the Calendar (Month, Week, and Agenda views) and can be completed or rescheduled in place.
   - Unscheduled Todo items (`due_at === null`) appear in an **Unscheduled To-dos** drawer next to the calendar so users can 1-click schedule any Todo onto a date/time slot.
   - Any calendar event can be promoted into a Todo item in `public.todo_items`, and `TodoWorkspace.svelte` (`/me/todos`) gains inline due-date scheduling, 1-click **Add to Calendar** (`Google`, `Outlook`, `M365`, `.ics`), and a top navigation bar switching between **To-dos & Wishlists** and **Calendars & Schedule**.
3. **Outbound Live `.ics` / `webcal://` Subscription Feed (`/api/calendars/feed.ics`)**:
   - Every user can generate/rotate/revoke a secret `.ics` / `webcal://` feed URL that streams their Webomage To-do deadlines (`VEVENT` + `VTODO`), scheduled interviews, and personal events into Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, Outlook, Proton Calendar, Notion Calendar, or any CalDAV/iCal client.
