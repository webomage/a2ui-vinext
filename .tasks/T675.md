# T675 — Project Role Templates (1-Head Indie vs 2-Head Default), Role/Hat Naming Matrix & Corporate vs RPG Theme

## Summary

Inspecting `Project Roles: PandaTransfer Exchange` (`https://cp.webomage.com/o/webomage/projects?side=contractor`) surfaced four architectural and UX issues in project role provisioning and terminology:

1. **Redundant `dev` Role on Standard 2-Head Projects & Missing `min_unit_id`**:
   - Standard delivery engagements (`delivery-min`) are designed around **2 contractor heads (`pm` + `tech-lead`)**, where `tech-lead` collapses into `pm` until staffed separately.
   - However, `T642` and `T665` seeded 3 roles (`pm`, `tech-lead`, `dev`) without copying `min_unit_id` from `public.role_templates`, and `public.wom_apply_collection` also omitted `min_unit_id`. As a result, `ProjectRolesModal.svelte` rendered `"No backing w0m unit (standalone delivery role)"` and two redundant `AUTO` fallback hats.
2. **Indie Projects Should Be 1-Head (`pm` Only) with a Better Lead Naming Alias**:
   - `INDIE_HATS` in `w0m-web/apps/cp/src/lib/project-types.ts` and `INDIE_ROLES` in `w0m-web/apps/cp/src/pages/api/project/roles.ts` provisioned 3 separate roles (`pm`, `tech-lead`, `dev`) for a solo builder.
   - An Indie / Solo template should deploy **only 1 contractor role (`slug: "pm"`)** with a clear lead naming alias (`Project Lead`, `Indie Builder`, `Product & Tech Lead`, or `Solo Founder` in Corporate mode; `Archmage Builder`, `Solo Artificer`, `Guild Master`, or `Realm Founder` in RPG mode).
3. **Registration-Time Template Selection via Naming Preset List + Automatic Trainee/Jobseeker Naming**:
   - Selecting from the naming preset list when registering a project (`ProjectManager.svelte`) or provisioning roles (`ProjectRolesModal.svelte`) should select whether to deploy the **1-Head Indie template (`pm` only)** or the **2-Head Default project template (`pm` + `tech-lead`)**, and automatically assign appropriate role/hat naming for **Trainees** (`trainee` / `trainer`) and **Jobseekers / Candidates** (`jobseeker`).
4. **Corporate (`web` = `"Role"`) vs RPG (`mage` = `"Hat"`) Terminology Theme**:
   - `SidebarHatSwitcher.svelte` and `ProjectRolesModal.svelte` hardcoded `"Hat"` (`Hat: ...`, `Project Hats`, `Take Off Project Hat`) even when the user's terminology theme (`T339`) was set to `"web"` (Classic Business / Corporate).
