# T607 — Implement Mexico professional self-accounting (RESICO 626 / Actividad Profesional 612) integrated with SAT, INM, and KYC checks

## Summary

Split from [[T604]] to enforce single-responsibility ticket hygiene:
- Implement Mexico professional self-accounting (`626 RESICO — Régimen Simplificado de Confianza` and `612 Persona Física con Actividades Empresariales y Profesionales`) in `@wom/kyc` (`w0m-web/packages/kyc/src/mexico/resico-inm.ts` and `w0m-web/packages/kyc/src/mexico-resico-accounting.ts`).
- Integrate SAT tax compliance checks (13-character Persona Física RFC, 18-character CURP, 18-digit CLABE with 3-2-7 modulo-10 checksum, Art. 113-E LISR `1.0%–2.5%` monthly ISR brackets, `$3.5M MXN` annual cap guard, Art. 113-J `1.25%` Mexican corporate client ISR retention, Art. 29 LIVA `0%` IT export IVA, `e.firma`, and `Opinión de Cumplimiento 32-D Positiva`) with INM immigration & work-authorization checks (`residente_permanente`, `residente_temporal_con_permiso`, NUT/NUE, card expiry) and the `@wom/kyc` verification scorecard.
- Expose `/me/accounting` (`ProfessionalSelfAccounting.svelte`) and `/api/profile/self-accounting-kyc` in `apps/cp`, connected to `/me/profile` (Resume Studio) and `/me/opportunities` (Project & Bounty Hunting).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] **AC1 — SAT `RESICO 626` & `Actividad Profesional 612` tax engine (`@wom/kyc`)**: Implement `calculateMexicoProfessionalTax`, `validateMexicanRfc`, `validateMexicanCurp`, and `validateMexicanClabe` in `@wom/kyc` covering Art. 113-E LISR monthly/annual brackets, `$3,500,000 MXN` RESICO cap enforcement, Art. 113-J `1.25%` ISR retention, and Art. 29 LIVA `0%` export IVA.
- [x] **AC2 — SAT + INM + KYC compliance eligibility evaluator (`@wom/kyc`)**: Implement `evaluateMexicoProfessionalCompliance` combining SAT (`RFC`, `e.firma`, `CSF`, `32-D Positiva`), INM work authorization (`citizen`, `residente_permanente`, `residente_temporal_con_permiso`, `NUE`/`NUT`, non-expired card), and KYC identity checks into a unified payout & project-hunting eligibility verdict.
- [x] **AC3 — Contractor Portal Self-Accounting UI (`/me/accounting`, `ProfessionalSelfAccounting.svelte`, `nav.ts`)**: Add `Self-Accounting (MX)` (`/me/accounting`) to `NAV.me` in `apps/cp/src/lib/context/nav.ts`, build `ProfessionalSelfAccounting.svelte` using `@webomage/ui` primitives, and connect it to `/me/profile` (Resume Studio) and `/me/opportunities` (Project Hunting).
