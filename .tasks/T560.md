# T560 — Move Resume Studio, Upwork sync, proposals, messages, recruiting, and contract management to job.webomage.com with 301 cutovers in cp.webomage.com

## Summary

Establish `job.webomage.com` (`wom7web/apps/job`) as the canonical portal for Member Career & Resume Studio, Freelancer/Marketplace Sync (Upwork), Proposals, Messages, Recruiting, and Contract Management across both member (`/me/*`) and organization (`/o/[org]/*`) scopes per `AgDR-0033`, while keeping `cp.webomage.com` (`wom7web/apps/cp`) strictly focused on Technical Project Management (`/o/[org]/projects/*`, `/o/[org]/p/[project]/*`), member delivery/identity core (`/me`, `/me/work`, `/me/oss`, `/me/todos`, `/me/profile`, `/me/account`), and `/admin/*` platform administration. All moved routes in `apps/cp` are cut over to deterministic 301 redirects to `job.webomage.com` with zero duplicate pages (`AgDR-0025`).
