## Acceptance criteria


- [ ] AC1 — **Audit produces a numbered drift list**, not an impression: token and component
      divergence between `design-system/` and `packages/ui` (`@webomage/ui`) plus `apps/*` CSS,
      enumerated in `Audit.dc.html` / `SpecVsCode.dc.html` / `GapComponents.dc.html`. A count of
      zero must be stated as a measured zero.
- [ ] AC2 — Every gap from AC1 is resolved or carries a written decision to keep the divergence.
      Silent non-resolution is the failure mode — an audit whose findings quietly expire.
- [ ] AC3 — Tokens and the component set are **locked in `design-system/`** as the source of truth,
      and the lock is dated.
- [ ] AC4 — ⚠️ **The sync direction is design-system → `packages/ui`, and both must agree at close.**
      These are two different things and confusing them is called out in `CLAUDE.md`:
      `design-system/` is the design source of truth, `packages/ui` is the live Svelte runtime. A
      component exists properly only when both carry it.
- [ ] AC5 — A UI spec authored after close cites the DS and passes the
      `SPEC-AUTHORING-STANDARD §Design system` check — proving the citation rule is enforced, not
      merely written down.
