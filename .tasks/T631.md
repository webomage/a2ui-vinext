# T631 — SuperPM & w0m (co-)owner role (`w0m.exec.owner`) for Aleksei & Elena with cross-project PM authority, transitive owner hat collapse, and project-scoped assigned-PM permissions

## Summary

Owner direction (`2026-11-03`):
1. **Strict separation between Platform Root `admin` (`w0m@webomage.com` / `casualuser@gmail.com`) and personal founder/co-owner accounts (`aleksei@webomage.com` + `elena@webomage.com`)**:
   - Keep `aleksei@webomage.com` (`8d16aff5-d996-4759-80b0-39b60e3c0920`) and `elena@webomage.com` (`9e901527-3901-45d5-bb04-909518e8ee20`) strictly separate from the institutional `admin` account (`public.user_roles.role = 'admin'` stays exclusive to `5691add5-48f9-4ac5-ab54-7ca5757e44da`; `/admin/*` on `cp.w0m.xyz` and `QH5` root-only capabilities `deploy.execute`, `infra.manage`, `secret.use.prod` stay exclusive to `admin`).
2. **First-class `SuperPM / w0m (Co-)Owner` role (`office = 'w0m.exec.owner'`, hat key `super_pm`) between `admin` and regular managers/users, assigned to BOTH Aleksei and Elena**:
   - Grant both `aleksei@webomage.com` and `elena@webomage.com` `user_roles` `'manager'` + `'member'` (updating `user_active_role` to `'manager'` so GoTrue's `wom_access_token_hook` projects `wom_active_role = 'manager'` at `aal2`) and an active, non-fallback estate-wide hat `(org_id = '00000000-0000-0000-0000-000000000001', office = 'w0m.exec.owner')` audited in `hat_assignment_log`.
   - Grant `SuperPM / w0m (Co-)Owner` extended cross-org & cross-project permissions across contractor/platform orgs (`w0m`, `webomage`, `sides = ['contractor']`) and **ALL** active projects (`wom_my_contexts`, `wom_context_capabilities`, `'owner'` authority in `wom_staff_authority` regardless of `projects.host_org_id`, on-demand contractor/delivery hat wearing via `wom_provision_owner_project_hat`, full engagement R2 vault access in `verify_vault_sync_token` + `vault-r2-gateway.ts`, and context-shared todo list access in `wom_can_access_todo_list`), while keeping **`client` hats (`client`, `client-*`, `client.*`) and client-portal side views strictly unavailable to `SuperPM / w0m (Co-)Owner` and available to Platform Root `admin` (and real client accounts) for now**.
3. **Full PM permissions for `admin`, `SuperPM`s (`aleksei` & `elena` across all projects), AND Assigned PMs (on their assigned projects)**:
   - Ensure assigned PMs (holders wearing an active non-fallback `pm` hat `w0m.tech.program` / `w0m.delivery.manager` on project `P`) have complete project-scoped PM permissions in SQL RLS and CP UI: project read/update, milestone read/create/edit/approve, contract read for project milestones, engagement vault read/push, and project team staffing (non-PM delivery hats per `QH1`, plus per-seat office toggles via `wom_toggle_project_office_grant`), while fixing `ProjectTeam.svelte` (`worn_role` / `assigned_hats` + server-verified `authority`) so controls render and execute cleanly.
4. **Transitive Hat Collapse to `w0m` Owners When No PM Is Assigned Yet (`dev`/`devops` → `tech-lead` → `pm` → `w0m.exec.owner`)**:
   - Upgrade `wom_recompute_project_fallbacks` and `wom_sync_role_fallback` (with triggers on `hats`, `project_roles`, and `hat_collapse_rules`) plus `ProjectTeam.svelte` (`getStandInInfo`) so that whenever a project has NO real `pm` assigned yet, `pm` and every unstaffed non-client hat collapse (`is_fallback = true`) to the active `w0m` owners (`aleksei` & `elena`). Once a real `pm` is assigned, owner fallbacks retract automatically and unstaffed delivery roles collapse to the assigned `pm` (or `tech-lead`). Client hats (`client`, `client-*`, `client.*`) NEVER collapse.
