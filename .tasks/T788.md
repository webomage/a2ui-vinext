# T788 — Support multiple SPEI accounts in LATAM self-accounting KYC and streamline Me to-dos navigation title

## Why

Mexican contractors and independent IT studios frequently maintain multiple bank accounts (e.g. BBVA corporate, Nu personal/savings, STP, Banorte) for tax segregation and disbursements. The previous self-accounting KYC form only permitted a single 18-digit CLABE input. Additionally, the personal hub sidebar and page header in `me.webomage.com` displayed "To-dos & wishlists", whereas the navigation item should simply read "To-dos" for clarity.

## Scope

- **In:**
  - Rename "To-dos & wishlists" to "To-dos" in `apps/me/src/lib/context/nav.ts`, `apps/me/src/pages/me/todos.astro`, and `apps/cp/src/components/todos/TodoWorkspace.svelte`.
  - Update `apps/cp/src/lib/context/__tests__/cp-context-menus-ac6.test.ts` and regenerate feature index (`just cp-feature-index`).
  - Add multi-account SPEI connection interface in `apps/cp/src/components/member/profile/ProfessionalSelfAccounting.svelte` with `+ Connect another SPEI account`, account alias, automatic bank detection, check-digit validation, and primary account selection.
  - Expand Mexican bank code dictionary in `packages/lib/src/payments/mexican-rails.ts` (Nu, Bancoppel, Azteca, Banregio, Inbursa, etc.).
  - Update `apps/cp/src/pages/api/profile/self-accounting-kyc.ts` to accept, validate, and persist `payoutAccounts` in `evaluation_snapshot` while storing the primary CLABE in `payout_account` for backward compatibility.
  - Author and update unit tests in `apps/cp/src/tests/api/profile/self-accounting-kyc.test.ts` and component tests in `ProfessionalSelfAccountingSpei.test.ts`.
  - Author visual user guide with screenshots for CP WASM tools at `w0m-web/docs/guides/CP-WASM-TOOLS-GUIDE.md`.
- **Out:**
  - Modifying non-LATAM payout rails or live third-party bank webhook integrations.
