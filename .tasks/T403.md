# T403 — Continuous ticket duplication check: automated similarity detector wired into SDLC gates and queue triage

## Summary

Owner directive, 2026-09-18: *"we have to enhance and start using continuously tickets duplication checks"*.

As the w0m ticket corpus expands past 400 tickets, duplicate tickets have entered the queue unnoticed (e.g. `T397` duplicating `T385`, `T398` duplicating `T382`, and `T399` duplicating paused `T99`). Previously, duplication checking relied entirely on manual memory and visual inspection of `TASKS.md`. There was zero automated verification to prevent duplicate scopes from being drafted or committed.

This ticket designs and implements an automated ticket duplication detector (`project/scripts/ticket_duplicate_check.py`), calculates lexical and token similarity (Jaccard on normalized word n-grams across title, summary, and acceptance criteria), supports candidate draft pre-flight checks, and continuously enforces duplication checks via `just ticket-dups`, `w0m-sdlc-new`, and pre-push quality gates.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] AC1 — **Author `project/scripts/ticket_duplicate_check.py`**: Implement a similarity checker across all tickets in `project/tickets/T*.md`. Evaluates weighted token and n-gram overlap across Title (0.45), Summary (0.35), and Acceptance Criteria (0.20), filtering stopwords and ignoring tickets already marked `closed_as: duplicate`.
- [x] AC2 — **Candidate Draft Pre-Flight Mode**: Support `--check-draft <file>` and `--check-ticket T<n>` to compare a proposed ticket against the entire active corpus before it is created, returning top matching tickets and similarity score.
- [x] AC3 — **Enforcement & Ratchet Gate**: Implement `--enforce` mode that exits non-zero if any active/queued ticket has a similarity score > 0.65 against another active ticket without an explicit `duplicate_of` or canonical relationship note. Add `just ticket-dups` and `just ticket-dups-test`.
- [x] AC4 — **Integrate into `w0m-sdlc-new` and `push_report.py`**: Wire `ticket_duplicate_check.py` into `w0m-sdlc-new` Step 4 (grounding) and surface duplication advisories in the Pre-Push Final Report (`push_report.py`).
- [x] AC5 — **Automated Test Suite**: Author `project/scripts/tests/test_ticket_duplicate_check.py` with synthetic test fixtures covering: (a) exact duplicates, (b) distinct tickets with shared domain words (no false alarm), (c) valid `closed_as: duplicate` exemptions, and (d) draft checking.
