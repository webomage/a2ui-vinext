## ⚠️ This is a PROGRAM, not a batch — and that distinction is deliberate

[[T200]] states the rule: *"the value of a batch is that its members share a surface, and that stops
being true at about five."* **These 13 tickets share a THEME, not an artefact.** Filing them as one
flat batch would break the rule six hours after it was written, and would produce a container whose
members cannot be worked together in any meaningful sense.

🔴 **[[T102]] and [[T117]] are NOT members of this program — [[T200]] owns them.** They surfaced in
the same scan because the office/capability catalogue is internal machinery, but **a ticket in two
batches means neither batch is authoritative.** T203 *depends on* T200's steps 3–4; it does not
re-own them.

⚪ **[[T171]] is excluded too.** The scan caught it, but a planner/scheduler for batched Claude Code
tasks in dev sandboxes is internal **tooling**, not the guild **mechanic**. Drawing that line is the
point of having a boundary at all.
