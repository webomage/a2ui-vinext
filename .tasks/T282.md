# T282: Compliance research & client audit service packets — TCPA, GDPR, SOC 2, Web3, license cleanliness, and technical audit offerings

## Context

Owner voice directive (`2026-08-25`, transcript items 1–2):
- Clients frequently have hidden compliance exposure (e.g., outbound calling / AI voice agents without TCPA consent tracking, healthcare apps without HIPAA safeguards, SaaS without GDPR/CCPA data deletion flows, enterprise B2B blocked on SOC 2 / ISO 27001, fintech touching card data under PCI-DSS).
- Beyond regulatory frameworks, Webomage can offer structured **technical & legal-technical audits**: open-source license cleanliness (copyleft/GPL contamination in proprietary repos), Web3 / smart-contract security reviews, cloud & database security posture, accessibility (WCAG / ADA), and AI safety / EU AI Act readiness.
- We need a structured research base and a **menu of productized audit / compliance readiness packets** that can be proposed to clients or attached to project blueprints.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] **AC1 — Regulatory compliance matrix**: Document the core frameworks relevant to our client base — TCPA (outbound voice/SMS & AI voice consent), GDPR / CCPA (privacy, DSAR, data residency), HIPAA (PHI handling, BAA requirements), PCI-DSS (payment flows), SOC 2 Type I/II & ISO 27001 (security controls), and EU AI Act / ISO 42001 — with clear triggers ("when does a client need this?") and penalty/risk profiles — implemented in [`wom7web/packages/lib/src/compliance/audit-catalog.ts`](wom7web/packages/lib/src/compliance/audit-catalog.ts) (`COMPLIANCE_FRAMEWORKS`) and [`wom7web/docs/backend/COMPLIANCE-AND-AUDIT-PACKETS.md`](wom7web/docs/backend/COMPLIANCE-AND-AUDIT-PACKETS.md).
- [x] **AC2 — Technical audit service catalog**: Define productized technical audit offerings — (a) Open-source license & IP cleanliness audit, (b) Web3 / smart-contract & wallet-flow security audit, (c) Cloud, RLS & zero-trust architecture audit, (d) WCAG / ADA accessibility audit, and (e) Outbound AI / telecom compliance audit — each with scope, deliverables, typical duration, and pricing tier — implemented in `TECHNICAL_AUDIT_SERVICES` (`oss_license_ip`, `web3_smart_contract`, `cloud_db_zero_trust`, `wcag_accessibility`, `ai_safety_aedt`) and `COMPLIANCE_FRAMEWORKS.readinessPacket` (`PKT-*`) in [`wom7web/packages/lib/src/compliance/audit-catalog.ts`](wom7web/packages/lib/src/compliance/audit-catalog.ts).
- [x] **AC3 — Capability vs. certification boundary**: Explicitly distinguish what Webomage delivers directly (technical readiness, remediation engineering, automated evidence collection, architecture hardening, pre-audit gap analysis) vs. what requires an accredited external auditor / legal counsel (formal SOC 2 attestation by a licensed CPA firm, ISO certification body, QSA, or formal legal opinion) — implemented in `CAPABILITY_TIERS` (`tier_1_direct`, `tier_2_partner_platform`, `tier_3_external_attestation`) and per-framework `webomageDirectDeliverables` vs `externalAttestationBoundary`.
- [x] **AC4 — Blueprint & proposal integration**: Package the audit offerings so they can be selected as standalone discovery engagements or add-on modules in the CP proposal / blueprint flow — implemented via `ReadinessPacketBlueprint` (`PKT-TCPA-10DLC`, `PKT-GDPR-CCPA`, `PKT-HIPAA-READY`, `PKT-PCI-SAQ-A`, `PKT-SOC2-ISO27001`, `PKT-EU-AI-42001`) and `calculateAuditEstimate(input)` in [`wom7web/packages/lib/src/compliance/audit-catalog.ts`](wom7web/packages/lib/src/compliance/audit-catalog.ts).
- [x] **AC5 — Client-facing positioning & intake checklist**: Provide a concise diagnostic questionnaire ("Compliance & Risk Quick-Scan") that sales / AI intake can use to flag which audit packets a lead or existing client needs — implemented in `AUDIT_INTAKE_QUESTIONNAIRE` (`Q1`–`Q7`) and `calculateAuditEstimate` in [`wom7web/packages/lib/src/compliance/audit-catalog.ts`](wom7web/packages/lib/src/compliance/audit-catalog.ts), verified by `packages/lib/src/compliance/audit-catalog.spec.ts` (5/5 passing).
