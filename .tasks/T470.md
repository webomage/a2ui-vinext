# T470 — Restore co-founder photos and executive titles on /team

## Summary

Owner direction (2026-09-27):
> "that's in most cases because we altered w0m unit slots but Aleksei goes to assigned to CEO/CTO slot and Elena to CFO/COO slot...
> and for Team page Option A is good right now but Option B is much better to follow finally"

Forensic analysis revealed:
1. In T198, public SELECT was dropped on `team_members` to stop staff email exposure.
2. In `TeamPage.astro:34`, public team queries execute as anon (`userClient()`), receiving `team_members: []`.
3. `hasMember` evaluates to `false`, causing `member.name` to fall back to `unit.title`.
4. In T443 & T445, Aleksei and Elena were assigned to foundational C-unit slots with titles `"Co-founder, CEO & CTO"` and `"Co-founder, CFO & COO"`.
5. In `TeamSection.astro`, `imageMap` keys were hardcoded to `"Aleksei Tcelishchev"` and `"Elena Shatalova"`, which failed to match the new titles or unit UUIDs, causing photos to be replaced with `/careers` placeholder silhouettes.

Resolution plan:
- **Phase 1 (Option A — immediate)**: Update `TeamPage.astro` and `TeamSection.astro` to map foundational founder unit IDs and titles to Aleksei and Elena's names, photos, CV/resume links, and bios.
- **Phase 2 (Option B — architectural SSOT)**: Author migration in `wom7api` creating public view `public_team_members` exposing safe columns (`id`, `name`, `bio`, `photo_url`, `status`, `unit_id`) without staff emails, restoring native relational queries under anon RLS.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] AC1 — **Option A Template Mapping**: Update `TeamPage.astro` so that when `team_members` returns empty under anon, known founder unit slots (`cfb52773-75f8-4f54-8dfa-422b7785d4e8` and `f84f7cad-bc19-46de-b397-ce9d1332cde3`) preserve names `"Aleksei Tcelishchev"` and `"Elena Shatalova"`, roles, bio facts, and CV/Resume links.

- [x] AC3 — **Option B Database Migration**: Author migration in `wom7api` introducing database VIEW `public_team_members` (unit_id, name, photo_url, summary, bio_facts, linkedin_url, status) with SELECT granted to anon and authenticated.
- [x] AC4 — **Verification**: Verify that `/team` renders both co-founders with photos, proper names, executive titles, and links in both dev and production prerender.
