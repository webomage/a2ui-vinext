# T644 — Expand `@wom/kyc`, Supabase identity/fiscal schema, and CP self-accounting to Costa Rica and 12 LATAM IT-hub countries

## Summary

Expand `@wom/kyc` (`w0m-web/packages/kyc`), Supabase identity & fiscal persistence (`w0m-api`), and Control Panel member/admin compliance flows (`w0m-web/apps/cp`) from Mexico-only self-accounting and payout gating to **12 LATAM IT-hub jurisdictions** (`CR` Costa Rica featured first-class alongside `MX`, `PA`, `GT`, `SV`, `DO`, `UY`, `BR`, `CO`, `AR`, `CL`, `PE`):

1. **12-Country `@wom/kyc` ID Validators & IT Fiscal/Tax/Migration/`W-8BEN` Engine**:
   - Add statutory check-digit validators and corporate/personal dispatchers for **Uruguay (`UY`)** (`RUT` 12-digit Mod 11, `CI` 7/8-digit Mod 10), **Dominican Republic (`DO`)** (`RNC` 9-digit DGII Mod 11, `Cédula` 11-digit Luhn Mod 10), **El Salvador (`SV`)** (`NIT` 14-digit MH, `DUI` 9-digit Mod 10), and **Guatemala (`GT`)** (`SAT NIT` Mod 11 `0-9/K`, `CUI/DPI` 13-digit RENAP), while upgrading **Costa Rica (`CR`)** with **NITE** (`10`-digit foreign tax ID) and **22-character `CR` SINPE IBAN** (`MOD 97 === 1`) validation.
   - Implement `w0m-web/packages/kyc/src/latam-fiscal.ts` (`LATAM_IT_TAX_CATALOG`, `calculateCostaRicaProfessionalTax`, `calculateLatamItFiscalSimulation`, `evaluateLatamFiscalKyc`) covering Costa Rica Hacienda (`Utilidades Ley 7092` progressive brackets, `PYME`, `Zona Franca PROCOMER Ley 7210`, `DGME Estancia Nómada Digital Ley 10008` with domestic-invoicing blocker, `CABYS 8314100000000` `0%` Ley 9635 IT export IVA vs `13%` domestic IVA, `CCSS Trabajador Independiente`, `SINPE IBAN`), all 11 sibling LATAM IT regimes (`UY` 100% IRPF software export exemption, `SV` Decreto 699 `0%` tech innovation, `PA` territorial `0%` foreign-source, `BR` Simples Anexo III Fator R, `CO` DIAN RST + PILA, `AR` Monotributo Tech + Factura E, `CL` SII BHE / Exportación Exenta + US-Chile Treaty, `PE` SUNAT RHE / MYPE, `DO` DGII RST / Zona Franca, `GT` SAT FEL `5%`, `MX` RESICO 626 / 612), and cross-border US/international `W-8BEN` / `W-8BEN-E` + `IRC §862(a)(3)` / US treaty readiness.
2. **Eliminate Non-MX Contractor KYC & Payout Block + Durable Audited Fiscal Persistence (`w0m-api`)**:
   - Extend `public.profile_identity` with `tax_country` and `tax_id` (plus `'cedula'` and `'dimex'` in `profile_identity_document`), self-invalidate `identity_verified_at` and biometric anchors in `public.profile_identity_guard()` when `tax_country` or `tax_id` mutates, and update `gamification.is_member_kyc_payout_eligible()`, `gamification.compliance_profiles_identity_guard()`, `gamification-economy`, `process-payout`, `kyc-verify.ts`, and `TreasuryManager.svelte` so verified non-MX LATAM contractors are first-class payout-eligible.
   - Create `public.profile_fiscal_profiles` (owner-only RLS + `wom_admin_reads_personal_data()` admin read) paired with append-only `public.profile_fiscal_audit_log` and `SECURITY DEFINER` trigger `public.wom_trg_profile_fiscal_audit()` (`T613`, `T614`).
3. **End-to-End Control Panel & Member Self-Accounting Workbench (`apps/cp` & `@webomage/lib`)**:
   - Wire `tax_country`, `tax_id`, `cedula`, and `dimex` in `w0m-web/packages/lib/src/profile/personal-info.ts` and `PersonalInformation.svelte`.
   - Upgrade `/api/profile/self-accounting-kyc` (`GET` + `POST` persistence) and `ProfessionalSelfAccounting.svelte` (`/me/accounting`) to support all 12 LATAM countries with `@webomage/ui` primitives, preserving legacy `MX` test IDs and API payload compatibility, and expose all 12 countries in `CorporateVerification.svelte` and `/api/admin/corporate-verify.ts`.
