# T604 — Power free website security audit service with Cloudflare security-audit-skill, enforce 1-free-audit quota with Simroute-grade smart visitor fingerprinting, surface results immediately in Client UI Deliveries on registration/login, and connect all main website workflows to registration & AI service ordering

## Summary

Owner P0 direction (`2026-11-01`):
1. **Use Cloudflare's `cloudflare/security-audit-skill` repo for our Free Website Security Audit service**:
   - Fork `cloudflare/security-audit-skill` to `github.com/webomage/security-audit-skill`, vendor `skills/security-audit` into `w0m-skills/skills/security-audit/`, and upgrade `@webomage/lib/security/passive-audit.ts` so every free website scan produces findings and a coverage ledger conforming to Cloudflare's `security-audit` machine-readable `report-schema.json` (`verdict`, `fingerprint`, `title`, `description`, `root_cause`, `intended_behavior`, `trace`, `evidence`, `conditions`, `execution`, `impact`, `severity`, `remediation`).
2. **Provide 1 Free Audit Request & Prevent Multiple Trial Registrations via Simroute-Grade Smart Visitor Fingerprinting**:
   - Update `@webomage/lib/entitlements/index.ts` so `trial` and `standard` tiers carry `security_audit_level1: 1` (1 free audit request) and `evaluateServiceEntitlement` enforces `used >= quota` when `quota !== -1`.
   - Upgrade `@webomage/fingerprint` (`packages/fingerprint/src/index.ts`) and `@webomage/lib/security/visitor-fingerprint.ts` with Simroute-reverse-engineered smart visitor fingerprinting (`sfp_<sha256>` combining WebGL unmasked vendor/renderer `37445`/`37446`, hardware vectors, timezone/locale consistency, automation/headless detection `navigator.webdriver`/`SwiftShader`/`window.chrome.runtime`, trust score `0.0–1.0`, and canonical email normalization stripping `+tag` / Gmail dot-aliases and blocking disposable domains) to prevent multiple free trial registrations or free audit farming across `/api/security-audit`, `/api/client/security-audit`, and `w0m-api/supabase/functions/client-register/index.ts`.
3. **Provide Results Immediately in Client UI Deliveries Upon Registration & Login**:
   - When a client registers after requesting a website audit (`POST /api/security-audit` `action: "register"` -> `submit-service-request` -> `client-register`) and logs into the Client Portal (`/client/portal` -> `/o/[org]`):
     - Persist the completed audit report (`latest_security_audit`), submitted service requests (`service_requests`), `visitor_fingerprint`, and `security_audit_level1: 1` quota usage onto `orgs.service_usage` and `leads` in `client-register/index.ts`.
     - Promote `Deliveries` / `Projects` (`path: "projects"`, `sides: BOTH`) and `Security Audit` (`path: "projects/security-audit"`, `sides: BOTH`) into `NAV.org` in `apps/cp/src/lib/context/nav.ts` (instead of hiding `security-audit` only in `DEEP_LINKS`).
     - Render the client's completed **Free Website Security Audit Delivery** (Grade, Score, CF `security-audit` verified findings badge, coverage ledger summary, `"1/1 Free Audit Used"`, and `"Open Full Audit Deliverable →"`) and submitted service requests immediately inside **Projects & Deliverables (`Deliveries`)** in `ClientDashboard.svelte`, `o/[org]/index.astro`, `o/[org]/projects/index.astro`, and `o/[org]/projects/security-audit.astro`.
4. **Main Website (`apps/web`) Ordering Visibility & Connect All User Workflows to Registration / AI Agent Service Ordering**:
   - Surface `Free Audit` (`/audit`) and `"Order Service"` in `Header.astro`, and embed the Free Website Audit & Service Ordering CTA on `HomePage.astro` and `ServicesPage.astro`.
   - Connect `HomeServices.astro`, `ServicesDetailed.astro`, `SolutionsGrid.astro`, `BlueprintGallery.astro`, `PageCTA.astro`, and `ContactFormSection.astro` to `ChatApp.svelte` (`wom:chat-order-service` / `wom:chat-open` custom events with pre-populated service/track/stage/blueprint order card + instant Client Portal registration handoff) and `client-register` per `openspec/changes/2026-07-11-client-conversion-and-contracts`.
