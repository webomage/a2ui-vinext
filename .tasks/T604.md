# T604 — Power free website security audit service with Cloudflare security-audit-skill and surface completed deliverables immediately in Client UI Deliveries

## Summary

Owner P0 direction (`2026-11-01`, narrowed to single-responsibility security-audit & Client Portal Deliveries scope on `2026-11-02`):
1. **Use Cloudflare's `cloudflare/security-audit-skill` repo for our Free Website Security Audit service**:
   - Fork `cloudflare/security-audit-skill` to `github.com/webomage/security-audit-skill`, vendor `skills/security-audit` into `w0m-skills/skills/security-audit/`, and upgrade `@webomage/lib/security/passive-audit.ts` so every free website scan produces findings and a coverage ledger conforming to Cloudflare's `security-audit` machine-readable `report-schema.json` (`verdict`, `fingerprint`, `title`, `description`, `root_cause`, `intended_behavior`, `trace`, `evidence`, `conditions`, `execution`, `impact`, `severity`, `remediation`).
2. **Enforce 1-Free-Audit Entitlement Quota**:
   - Update `@webomage/lib/entitlements/index.ts` so `trial` and `standard` tiers carry `security_audit_level1: 1` (1 free audit request) and `evaluateServiceEntitlement` enforces `used >= quota` when `quota !== -1`.
3. **Provide Results Immediately in Client UI Deliveries Upon Registration & Login**:
   - When a client registers after requesting a website audit (`POST /api/security-audit` `action: "register"` -> `submit-service-request` -> `client-register`) and logs into the Client Portal (`/client/portal` -> `/o/[org]`):
     - Persist the completed audit report (`latest_security_audit`), `deliveries`, and `security_audit_level1: 1` quota usage onto `orgs.service_usage` and `leads` in `client-register/index.ts`.
     - Promote `Deliveries` / `Projects` (`path: "projects"`, `sides: BOTH`) and `Security Audit` (`path: "projects/security-audit"`, `sides: BOTH`) into `NAV.org` in `apps/cp/src/lib/context/nav.ts`.
     - Render the client's completed **Free Website Security Audit Delivery** (Grade, Score, CF `security-audit` verified findings badge, coverage ledger summary, `"1/1 Free Audit Used"`, and `"Open Full Audit Deliverable →"`) immediately inside **Projects & Deliverables (`Deliveries`)** in `ClientDashboard.svelte`, `o/[org]/index.astro`, `o/[org]/projects/index.astro`, `o/[org]/projects/security-audit.astro`, and `SecurityAuditReportViewer.svelte`.
