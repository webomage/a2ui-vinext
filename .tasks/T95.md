      inventory of connected services is stale the week after it is written; this estate's recurring
      defect is a check that only looks where it already knows to look. Either a recipe reports
      declared-vs-actual, or the doc states plainly that it is a point-in-time snapshot and dates it.
- [x] AC5 — The output is consumable by its two declared downstreams — the T96 audit and the T94
      registry — rather than being a document only humans read.
