# T616 — Verify completed tickets and specs against live codebase/deploy reality and restore 100% green offline test suites

## Summary

Auditing completed (`status: done`) tickets, OpenSpec changes/specs, and the full `302`-suite offline matrix exposed two coupled structural blind spots:

1. **Completed tickets and specs drifted silently from current estate reality:**
   - `scripts/dod_check.py` still pointed at retired `w0m-web/project/tickets` (`0` files) and silently exited `0` (`[OK]`) without inspecting `.tasks/T*.md`.
   - `scripts/spec_drift_gate.py` failed to strip inline `#` comments on YAML frontmatter (`status: done # ...`), misclassifying closed owners as open; **9 of 10** active `w0m-web/openspec/changes/*` had 100% `done` ticket owners while remaining unarchived.
   - `scripts/ticket_audit.py` only inspected single tickets on demand, resolved neither app-shorthand paths nor exported TypeScript/Python symbols, SQL relations, or `releases/*.json` deployment state (`deployed` vs `landed_pending_deploy`), and never scanned canonical `w0m-web/openspec/specs/**/*.md`.
2. **`just test` defaulted to `W0M_TEST_LEVEL=critical` (`14` prepush suites), masking `30` failing blocking offline suites plus worktree provisioning gaps and `3` uncounted (`?`) blocking suites:**
   - Running `just test-full` (`302` offline suites) revealed `30` failing blocking suites, `3` uncounted blocking suites (`hooks-llm-policy`, `cloud-compute-check`, `consumer-skill-gate-test` — the latter a silent `0`-test no-op running `python3` on a `pytest` module), and `4` member-repo `node_modules` worktree provisioning gaps in `scripts/release.py`.
