# T688 — Accelerate session-complete and agent-tree landing (auto-resolve w0m-web derived docs, decouple graph rebuild lock, and fast-path certification)

## Summary

Concurrent agent trees queued for 15–23m on `w0m-session-complete.lock` because:
1. `w0m-web` derived docs (`docs/CODEMAP.md`, `docs/CP-FEATURE-INDEX.md`) were not reset in `sync_worktree_onto_origin_beta()` or auto-regenerated on rebase conflicts in `w0m-web/project/scripts/agent_tree.py`.
2. Post-GitHub-sync `harvest_and_refresh_surfaces()` re-ran `codemap_gen.py`, `cp_feature_index.py`, `catalog_sync.py`, and `skills_catalog_gen.py` even when only `.tasks/T##.md` frontmatter changed, and `land_and_cleanup_tree()` spawned a second `certify_estate_tree()` subprocess even when `agent_tree.py land` did not rebase or alter HEAD SHAs.
3. `refresh_canonical_graph_union()` (~44s) executed inside `with session_complete_lock(...)`, blocking waiting sessions after `origin/beta` push, canonical fast-forward, and `state_doctor.py --check` were already complete.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] AC1 — **Auto-resolve `w0m-web` derived files (`docs/CODEMAP.md` and `docs/CP-FEATURE-INDEX.md`):** Reset `docs/CODEMAP.md` and `docs/CP-FEATURE-INDEX.md` before `git stash push` / `git reset --hard origin/beta` in `scripts/session_complete.py:sync_worktree_onto_origin_beta()` and auto-regenerate `DERIVED_WEB_GENERATORS` (`docs/CODEMAP.md`, `docs/CP-FEATURE-INDEX.md`, `docs/SKILLS-CATALOG.md`, `project/catalog-entities.json`) during `w0m-web` rebase conflicts in `w0m-web/project/scripts/agent_tree.py`.
- [x] AC2 — **Decouple post-land graph union rebuild from `w0m-session-complete.lock`:** Defer `refresh_canonical_graph_union()` until after `with session_complete_lock(...)` exits and serialize post-lock graph rebuilds under `w0m-graph-rebuild.lock` so queued sessions acquire `w0m-session-complete.lock` immediately after `state_doctor.py --check`.
- [x] AC3 — **Fast-path ticket-only surface refresh & unchanged-SHA post-land certification:** Add `tickets_only=True` to post-GitHub-sync `harvest_and_refresh_surfaces()`, reuse pre-land certification when `pre_land_shas == post_land_shas` in `land_and_cleanup_tree()`, and cover all three speedups in `scripts/tests/test_session_complete.py`.

## Definition of Done
