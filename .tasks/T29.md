role.

## Status notes

🟢 **Done (2026-10-20, 4/4 ACs).**
- **AC1 (Blueprint acceptance provisions private repos under agency GH org):** `wom7api/supabase/functions/lead-api/handler.ts` (`accept_proposal`) now creates/links the `public.projects` workspace, provisions/records private component repositories (`provider = 'github'`, `owner = 'webomage'`, `visibility = 'private'`) per blueprint component in `public.project_repos` (`onConflict: "project_id,provider,owner,name"`) using a GitHub App installation token (`GITHUB_APP_INSTALLATION_TOKEN`), assigns the PM as `owner` in `public.project_acl`, and is idempotent on retry.
- **AC2 (PM-owned board seeded with epics/tasks from blueprint, multi-repo per project):** `buildDeliveryBoard` seeds `projects.metadata.delivery_board` with epics derived from the blueprint's active variants and multi-repo tasks tagged by `repo_role` and `repo_name` (`webomage/<project-slug>-<role>`), along with 1:1 linked `public.client_milestones` rows.
- **AC3 (Board ↔ contract/milestone sync):** Added two-way `sync_delivery_milestone` action in `lead-api` and wired `approve_milestone` / `request_revision` in `wom7api/supabase/functions/client-api/index.ts` to keep `public.client_milestones` (`pending`, `delivered`, `approved`, `rejected`) and `delivery_board` epics/tasks (`todo`, `in_progress`, `in_review`, `done`, `changes_requested`) in lockstep while recording and surfacing any `sync_error` (`502`).
- **AC4 (Quest- and WebMCP-operable & client visibility):** When `projects.gamification_enabled` is true, delivery tasks expose Model A `quest_bounty` descriptors (`AgDR-0022`). Added `get_project_delivery` (returning full board + `quest_bounties` for internal members/admins, sanitized own-linkage `progress_summary` for owning clients, and `403` on cross-tenant access) plus audited `grant_client_repo_access` / `revoke_client_repo_access`, and registered `get_project_delivery` and `sync_delivery_milestone` in `@webomage/webmcp` (`wom7web/packages/webmcp/src/manifest.ts` & `roles.ts`). Verified by `wom7api/supabase/functions/lead-api/lead-api.test.ts` (23/23 passing) and `wom7web/packages/webmcp` tests.
