# T528 — KYC personal half

## Summary

Split from [[T505]] AC7 and AC11. [[T505]] built the member's contact record (`profile_contact`) and a separate locked identity record (`profile_identity`). Two things it said belonged to "the T349 personal half" have no owner: [[T349]] closed 7 of 7 criteria on the
business half only, and its own note calls the member half "a rubber stamp, not a real check". The only other open KYC ticket is [[T475]] (the biometric hardware anchor).

**What is open:**
- **Read the records.** KYC must read `profile_contact` (not its own copy) for phone and address verification, and `profile_identity` for legal name, birth date, CURP and RFC.
- **Move CURP and RFC.** They sit in `gamification.compliance_profiles` (0 rows, keyed by login, not profile). It is read by `apps/cp/src/pages/api/admin/kyc-verify.ts`, `TreasuryManager.svelte`, the `gamification-economy` edge function and `project/scripts/t349_kyc_probe.py`,
  so moving them changes compliance and payout gating: it needs a reading of each reader, not a search and replace.
- **Decide providers.** Phone OTP and proof of address each need a provider. That is a spending and vendor choice, so it is the owner's.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] AC1 — **Providers decided** (owner): the phone OTP provider and the proof-of-address provider, recorded as an AgDR with the cost and the data each one receives.
- [x] AC2 — **KYC reads `profile_contact`** for phone and address verification; the verification markers are written by KYC alone and shown read-only in Profile (as [[T505]] built), and no second copy of the phone or address remains.
- [x] AC3 — **CURP and RFC live on `profile_identity`**, keyed by profile; every reader of `gamification.compliance_profiles` (the four named above) reads the new place, with a test each, and the old columns are dropped in the same change (no shim, AgDR-0025).
- [x] AC4 — **Payout gating is unchanged where it should be, and fails closed where data is missing.** A member with no identity record is refused, never passed; proven by a probe against a scratch database with the real policies.
- [x] AC5 — **Live proof** (owner-run after the migration): a verified phone and address show as verified in Profile, a changed phone clears its marker, and another member cannot read either record.
