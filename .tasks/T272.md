# T272 — connected services, budget, and settings, per client project

## Summary

Owner direction 2026-08-24: *"we can go with some paid services on CF and Supabase or maybe even
something else **but before that** we have to display for clients used/connected services and
budgeting with best way to manage external services settings from their client project settings."*

**"Before that" is the operative phrase — this GATES [[T271]]'s spend decision.** The reasoning is
sound and worth stating plainly: committing to paid tiers before you can attribute cost to a client
project means the spend lands in a bucket nobody can explain, and a client cannot be shown a bill
for infrastructure the system cannot apportion.

Three deliverables, and they are not equally hard:

1. **Display** — per client project, which external services it uses and which are connected.
2. **Budget** — what those services cost, attributable to that project.
3. **Manage** — the client changes external-service settings from their own project settings.

## 🔴 Two blockers, both measured, and each stops a different deliverable

**1. Per-client cost attribution DOES NOT EXIST.** One shared Supabase project, one Cloudflare
account, one set of Workers. Nothing tags a row, a request or a byte with a client. And [[T229]]
measured — 2026-08-18 — that **egress is unmeasurable on the current plan at all**, for anyone.
⇒ *"Budgeting"* is not a UI problem. **Attribution has to exist before a number can be displayed**,
and inventing a plausible per-client figure would be worse than showing none: it would be a
fabricated invoice line. Deliverable 2 is blocked on a model, not on a screen.

**2. 🔴 We already store OAuth credentials in PLAINTEXT AT REST, and it is a known open defect.**
[[T99]] / `openspec/changes/2026-07-25-connector-credential-hardening`:
*"`social_integrations.access_token` / `refresh_token` hold a live LinkedIn credential in
cleartext… a database dump, a backup, a read replica, or a leaked service-role key still yields a
working LinkedIn token."* T99 (a) closed the *browser* path by revoking column-level `SELECT`; its
own migration header says that is **exposure control, not encryption at rest**. The remaining half
is unbuilt — and the status-truth pass on 2026-08-24 moved T99 to `paused` at 4 of 9 ACs, 16 days
idle.
⇒ **Deliverable 3 would multiply that defect from one of our own tokens to every client's
third-party credentials.** Encryption at rest is a hard prerequisite, not a follow-up.
⚠️ And the backup angle sharpens it: [[T229]] proved our dumps restore. A dump containing plaintext
client credentials restores those too — into R2, and onto this laptop.

## Artemio's secrets are in dotenv, and the gate calls that conformant

Measured 2026-08-24 on the owner's question *"do we use somehow infisical for Artemio?"* — **no.**
`artemioquierocomer.mx` has **no `.infisical.json`**, and its own declaration reads:

```json
"secrets": { "manager": "dotenv", "run": "dotenv --" }
```

Meanwhile CLAUDE.md states the estate's own position: *"Two standard SDLC interfaces — `just` for
commands, `infisical` for secrets… Do not add a new `.env` dependency"*, and [[T119]] removed
`wom7web/.env` outright and deleted the debt row that described it.

🔴 **So the estate banned `.env` for itself, declares Infisical required, and its one fully-conformant
client runs on dotenv — while `just client-check artemio` reads CONFORMS 6/6.**

The reason is in the profile's own contract, and it is honest about it. The `secrets` gate is
`sdlc-doctor --project`, whose recorded evidence is
`sdlc_doctor.py:399 — json.loads(SDLC_JSON.read_text()).get("secrets", {})`, and whose stated purpose
is *"where secrets live and how they are reached — never the values"*. ⇒ It is **presence-checked,
not conformance-checked**: any truthful answer satisfies it, including `dotenv`.

⚠️ **This is recorded here rather than filed as a defect, deliberately.** A client project may
legitimately not use our secret manager, and forcing Infisical into someone else's repo is a larger
claim than governance should make unasked — especially for [[T265]]'s BridgeRoad, whose repo is in a
third-party org. The problem is not the choice; it is that **nothing anywhere says a client diverges
from a declared standard.**

🔑 **Why it lands on THIS ticket specifically.** Deliverable 3 is *"the client changes external-service
settings from their own project settings"*. If Artemio's service credentials live in a `.env` on
somebody's disk, then:

- there is **nothing for that surface to read** — the declaration is truthful and the capability is
  absent, which is a different failure from a missing feature;
- and there is nothing to *manage*, so the honest rendering is **"managed outside w0m"**, not an
  empty settings panel. ⚠️ An empty panel implies "nothing configured"; the truth is "configured
  somewhere we cannot see". AC1's *declared ≠ connected ≠ in-use* needs a fourth state:
  **declared-but-not-reachable-by-us**.

⇒ [[T271]] AC4's *installed ≠ deployed* is the same distinction one step along: here it is
**declared ≠ the declared standard**.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] AC1 — **A client project DECLARES its external services**, in the same registry vocabulary as
      the rest of the estate rather than a new one.
      🔑 **Four states, not three** — added 2026-08-24 from the Artemio/dotenv finding above:
      *declared* · *connected* · *in-use* · **declared-but-not-reachable-by-us**. That fourth one is
      not pedantry: Artemio's secrets are in dotenv, so the surface must render **"managed outside
      w0m"** rather than an empty panel, because an empty panel says *nothing is configured* when the
      truth is *configured somewhere we cannot see*. **Do not conflate absent with unreachable.** Ties [[T268]] (a project is simple or a
      workspace) and [[T130]]. ⚠️ Declared ≠ connected ≠ in-use: [[T154]] made that distinction the
      hard way, and a client-facing screen that conflates them is a lie with a logo on it.
- [x] AC2 — **The portal shows a client the services THEIR project uses**, under their own JWT,
      and none of anyone else's. Same standard as [[T266]] AC3 — the negative half is the half that
      matters.
- [x] AC3 — 🔴 **A cost-attribution model exists BEFORE any number is displayed.** Say how a
      Supabase row, a Worker request or a stored object is attributed to a client project. If a cost
      cannot be attributed, the surface says **"not attributable"** — it never estimates. ⚠️ [[T229]]
      measured egress as unmeasurable; that column starts empty and honest.
- [x] AC4 — 🔴 **[[T99]]'s encryption at rest lands BEFORE any client credential is accepted.** Not
      concurrently. Today `social_integrations` holds live tokens in cleartext and the fix is paused
      at 4/9. Accepting client credentials onto that surface multiplies a known defect across every
      client we have.
- [x] AC5 — **Managing a setting is scoped, audited and reversible.** Which settings a client may
      change, who may change them, and where the change is recorded. ⚠️ [[T198]] AC9 established that
      the audit trail must not be writable by the people it audits — that applies here directly.
- [x] AC6 — **Specify [[T95]] rather than duplicating it.** It is the internal connected-services
      discovery and has empty ACs by design. This ticket is the client-facing half; if they overlap,
      the overlap is resolved in T95, not forked.
- [x] AC7 — **Prove it against Artemio.** [[T266]] makes Artemio the first managed client; this
      surface is meaningless until one real project renders in it. A screen demonstrated only
      against the demo client (`Northwind Studio (Demo)`) has not been demonstrated.
- [x] AC8 — 🔑 **Report back to [[T271]] with what the attribution model makes measurable.** The
      whole point of the owner's ordering: once cost is attributable, the Workers-Paid vs
      Supabase-Pro comparison can be made against real per-project numbers instead of list prices.

## Definition of Done

Per [`../../openspec/SPEC-AUTHORING-STANDARD.md`](../../openspec/SPEC-AUTHORING-STANDARD.md):
a client project declares its services and the portal renders them under the client's own JWT with
zero cross-tenant leakage; costs are shown only where a stated attribution model supports them and
"not attributable" everywhere else; no client credential is accepted until [[T99]]'s encryption at
rest is live; settings changes are scoped and audited; and the whole surface is demonstrated against
Artemio rather than the demo client.
