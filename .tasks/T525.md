# T525 — Admin CP rework, after the owner confirms the classification

## Summary

[[T493]] moved what was clearly personal into the member area (Me) and deliberately left the admin console untouched as the reference until the owner confirms. This is the step that follows that confirmation.
`project/cp-view-dispositions.json` records, for every org and admin view, whether it stays, has moved, is retired, or is still waiting for the owner. At the time of writing 10 of the 50 views are waiting
(`proposed-move`, `review` or `unclassified`); this ticket cannot start on them until the owner decides, and it must not decide for them.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC1 — **Nothing is waiting.** Every row in `project/cp-view-dispositions.json` is `kept-in-admin`, `kept-in-org`, `moved` or `retired`: zero `proposed-move`, `review` or `unclassified`. Verified by `just cp-feature-index`, whose summary shows "Waiting for the owner: 0".
- [ ] AC2 — **Each `moved` view exists in Me.** A member route with the same data, scoped to the caller's own profile, a nav item, a feature-index row and a test; the old org or admin view is gone from the nav and its row stays as `moved` with `me_path`.
- [ ] AC3 — **Each `retired` view is removed.** Nav item and page deleted, its row kept as `retired` with its label and a note saying where its job went.
- [ ] AC4 — **The legacy `/` System Overview is retired.** Its six widgets (HealthDashboard, ConnectivityStatus, ScraperDashboard, SecretManager, DomainManager, AIContentAssistant) are mounted under `/admin` Overview, and `/` redirects. The feature index no longer lists `/` under "Pages no nav item reaches".
- [ ] AC5 — **Admin holds only w0m system management.** Every admin-context view is `kept-in-admin`, and `just cp-nav-check --enforce` and the CP goldens pass (goldens re-baselined deliberately, not to go green).
