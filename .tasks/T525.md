# T525 — Admin CP rework, after the owner confirms the classification

## Summary

[[T493]] moved what was clearly personal into the member area (Me) and deliberately left the admin console untouched as the reference until the owner confirms. This is the step that follows that confirmation.
`project/cp-view-dispositions.json` records, for every org and admin view, whether it stays, has moved, is retired, or is still waiting for the owner. At the time of writing 10 of the 50 views are waiting
(`proposed-move`, `review` or `unclassified`); this ticket cannot start on them until the owner decides, and it must not decide for them.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] AC1 — **Nothing is waiting.** Every row in `project/cp-view-dispositions.json` is `kept-in-admin`, `kept-in-org`, `moved` or `retired`: zero `proposed-move`, `review` or `unclassified`. Verified by `just cp-feature-index`, whose summary shows "Waiting for the owner: 0".
- [N/A] AC2 — **Each `moved` view exists in Me.** Not taken: the two approved moves did not hold up (see the 2026-10-01 note) and the owner decided on 2026-10-01 that the CV generator is preserved for now and Blueprints stay admin-managed with provisioning in engagement setup ([[T534]]). No view is `moved`.

- [N/A] AC3 — **Each `retired` view is removed.** Not taken: the owner kept the CV generator for now. No view is `retired`; it is revisited when it is clear whether to replace or implement it.

- [x] AC4 — **The legacy `/` System Overview is retired.** Its six widgets (HealthDashboard, ConnectivityStatus, ScraperDashboard, SecretManager, DomainManager, AIContentAssistant) are mounted under `/admin` Overview, and `/` redirects. The feature index no longer lists `/` under "Pages no nav item reaches" as a widget-mounting page (`components == []`, verified by `test_cp_feature_index.py`).
- [x] AC5 — **Admin holds only w0m system management.** Every admin-context view is `kept-in-admin`, and `just cp-nav-check --enforce` and the CP goldens pass (goldens re-baselined deliberately, not to go green).

## Status notes

- 2026-10-01 — Filed from [[T493]] AC6. Blocked on the owner's classification review (T493 AC3).

- 2026-10-01 (research before building, read-only) — **Both approved moves rest on premises the data does not support; nothing was built.** The moves were approved from the T493 table's one-line descriptions ("blueprints I authored", "CV generator overlaps Resume studio").
  **Blueprints:** `public.blueprints` has 27 rows (24 `public`, 3 `internal`) and **no author or owner column**; no table links a blueprint to a profile. Write access is the capability `blueprint.publish` (policy "Blueprint writers hold capability", ALL) and reads are public. So "blueprints I authored" cannot be shown: there is no authorship to filter on. A Me view could only be "blueprints I may edit", which is the org editor again.
  **CV generator:** it is a recruiting tool for CANDIDATES, not the member's own CV, and it is dead on three counts. (1) Its page queries `profile_snapshots` joined to `users`, and `public.users` does not exist; `profile_snapshots` has 1 row. (2) Its button posts to `https://api.webomage.com/api/generate/cv`: that host has no DNS record, and no repo defines the route. (3) The `CvGeneratorWorkflow` in `wom7api/src/workflows/cv-generator.ts` (candidate LinkedIn snapshot to a tailored headline and summary) needs `GEMINI_API_KEY`, which Google disabled on 2026-09-30. Moving it into Me would also expose applicant data to every member.
  **Recommended dispositions (owner decides; `project/cp-view-dispositions.json` still says `proposed-move` for both):** CV generator `retired` (delete the nav item, page and `CVGenerator.svelte`; keep the workflow and table for a separate recruiting decision); Blueprints stays `kept-in-org` unless the owner wants authorship recorded first (a column plus a backfill: to whom?).

- 2026-10-01 (owner decision) — **The two moves are cancelled.** CV generator: preserved for now, until it is clear whether to replace it with something or implement it; `kept-in-org` in `project/cp-view-dispositions.json`. Blueprints: in general available for management by the admin only, and provisioning a blueprint with selected technologies and activated tech chips belongs in engagement setup, so the research and spec is [[T534]] (`Split from: [[T525]]`). With both rows decided the feature index shows **0 waiting**, so AC1 is met by the file; AC4 and AC5 (retire the legacy `/` System Overview, admin holds only system management) remain, and the owner's AC3 review from [[T493]] is complete.
