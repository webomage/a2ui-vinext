# T614 — Wire end-to-end regular-user public profiles (`/u/<slug>`, `/u/<slug>/cv`, `/co/<slug>`, `cv-pdf?slug=`), eliminate synthetic 200 fallbacks, and add append-only `profile_public_audit_log`

## Summary

Auditing public profile and CV wiring across `w0m-api` and `w0m-web` (`T494`/`T510` follow-through) exposed 7 unwired surfaces and silent fallbacks preventing regular users from publishing a public profile or CV and leaving public-visibility mutations unaudited:
1. **`ProfilePrivacyControls.svelte` wrote undeclared column `public_visibility`**: `public.profile_public_settings` (`20261001060000_t510_resume_publication.sql`) only defined `(profile_id, resume_published, show_linkedin_on_card, updated_at)` with column-scoped `authenticated` grants, so upserting `public_visibility` failed at runtime in PostgREST.
2. **No `public_slug`, `public_profile_enabled`, or `anon`-readable personal/company views**: Regular users had no slug or master opt-in toggle, `fetchPublicPersonalProfile` fell back to querying RLS-protected `profiles`/`profile_public_settings`/`profile_skills` as `anon` (always empty), and `fetchPublicCompanyProfile` queried `.from("organizations")` (a relation that does not exist; `public.orgs` is canonical).
3. **Dead `portfolio` & `connected_accounts` toggles**: `portfolio` and `connected_accounts` visibility checkboxes were stored in UI state without ever querying `profile_experience` or `connections` (`owner_kind = 'member'`).
4. **Synthetic `200 OK` placeholder fallbacks (`AgDR-0025` violation)**: `apps/web/src/pages/u/[slug].astro`, `u/[slug]/cv.astro`, and `co/[slug].astro` synthesized fake `200 OK` profiles for any unknown or private slug instead of returning `404 Not Found`.
5. **Team-only `published_resumes` & `cv-pdf`**: `published_resumes` and `w0m-api/supabase/functions/cv-pdf` accepted only `team_members.id` (`?member=<uuid>`), preventing regular users in `job.webomage.com/me/resume` from serving their Primary CV PDF at `?slug=<public_slug>`.
6. **Fragmented CP/Job controls & hardcoded team slugs**: `PublicSettings.svelte` hardcoded `"aleksei"` / `"elena"` slugs and raw `<button>` tags, while `/profile.astro` and `apps/job/src/pages/me/resume.astro` omitted `ProfilePrivacyControls.svelte` and `ResumeStudio.svelte` could not link a regular user's public `/u/<slug>/cv`.
7. **Zero audited change log on public-visibility mutations**: Unlike `client_service_audit_log`, `profile_public_settings` had no append-only `profile_public_audit_log` trigger or user-visible audit history.
