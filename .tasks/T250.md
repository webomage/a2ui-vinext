# T250 — The TASKS row and the ticket file disagree about status on 8 tickets

## Summary

`CLAUDE.md` says it in as many words: **"`TASKS.md` — the queue — the T-row is status-of-record."**
Measured 2026-08-21, the row and the ticket's own `status:` field disagree on **8 tickets**, in
**both** directions:

| ticket | `status:` in the ticket | the TASKS row says |
|---|---|---|
| T13 | `done` | ⚪ *"LIKELY ALREADY DELIVERED — verify and archive"* |
| T174 | `done` | 🟢 *"BUILD DONE 2026-08-07"* |
| T194 | `done` | 🟡 *"AC1–AC4 BUILT 2026-08-13"* |
| T211 | `done` | ⚪ *"filed 2026-08-14 — queued for implementation"* |
| T238 | `done` | 🟡 (no text at all) |
| T246 | `done` | 🔴 *"FIX WRITTEN, awaiting the owner"* — **fixed under [[T248]]** |
| T247 | `done` | 🟡 *"AC1+AC2 DONE"* — **fixed under [[T248]]** |
| T248 | `done` | 🔴 *"queued"* — **fixed under [[T248]]** |
| **T74** | **`queued`** | ✅ — **the inverse: a row claiming done over an open ticket** |

The three found mid-flight were repaired in [[T248]]'s commit because they were that session's own
work. The remaining **six** are the ticket, and they are not one person's oversight: they span
2026-08-07 to 2026-08-14 and four different authors' sessions.

🔑 **T74 is the one that should worry us.** Every other row *understates* progress, which is
merely stale. T74's row says ✅ over a ticket that is still `queued` — a **false green on the
surface CLAUDE.md designates as status-of-record.** Anyone reading the queue to pick work skips it.

## Why nothing caught it

[[T243]] built the **status ↔ ACs** gate: a ticket cannot claim `done` with unticked ACs. This is
its sibling and it does not exist — **status ↔ row**. The two surfaces are written in the same
commit by convention and by nothing else, and convention is not a gate. `just tasks` reads the
ticket files, `just tasks-bounds` checks the row *shape*; neither compares the two claims.

⇒ The same shape as [[T248]] and [[T247]] ③: **the data to catch this was sitting in two files the
whole time and nothing compared them.**
