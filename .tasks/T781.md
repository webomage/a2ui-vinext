# T781 — Publish webomage.com legal compliance pack, footer legal nav, unchecked consent + policy version tracking, and Stripe tax ID / legal metadata

## Summary

Addresses legal and checkout compliance gaps on `webomage.com` and shared portal footers:

1. **Public Legal Document Suite (`apps/web/src/pages/`):** Publishes `/terms`, `/refunds` (plus clean 301 alias `/cancellation`), `/dpa` (GDPR Art. 28 + live subprocessor table), `/sla`, `/cookies`, `/aviso-de-privacidad` (Spanish-language Mexican LFPDPPP *Aviso de Privacidad Integral* naming the **Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno (SABG)** after the March 20, 2025 DOF reform), refreshes `/privacy` (reconciling Microsoft Clarity & Google Analytics telemetry instead of the stale "no tracking cookies" claim), and links `/security` directly to `/terms`.
2. **Global Footer Legal Navigation:** Adds a compact, wrapped, short-viewport-safe legal navigation bar to both `apps/web/src/components/Footer.astro` and `packages/ui/src/lib/components/ui/SharedFooter.astro` while preserving `data-testid="canonical-footer-brand-mark"`.
3. **Explicit Opt-In Consent & Policy Version Recording:** Changes pre-checked `tosConsent = $state(true)` / `roeAttested = $state(true)` defaults to `$state(false)` across `ServiceActionOrderConfigurator.svelte`, `SecurityAuditWidget.svelte`, `BlueprintOrderFlow.svelte`, and `AuditPricing.svelte`, adds clickable links to `/terms`, `/privacy`, and `/refunds`, and forwards `tosPolicyVersion` (`"2026-11-20"`) + `tosAcceptedAt` through `POST /api/security-audit`.
4. **Stripe Checkout Tax ID & Legal Notice (`w0m-api`):** Updates `_shared/stripe-billing.ts` and `stripe-checkout/index.ts` (all 3 modes: audit, subscription, invoice) to enable `tax_id_collection: { enabled: true }`, `custom_text.submit.message` linking `/terms` and `/refunds`, and `metadata.tos_policy_version`.
