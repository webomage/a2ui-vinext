# T795 — Purge unused @tinybirdco/sdk to eliminate Zod v3, bump smol-toml override floor, and register D70 in TECH-DEBT

## Why

1. `w0m-web` ignored `GHSA-mrhx-c7xc-m3w5` (zod 3.x) because `@tinybirdco/sdk@0.0.66` was the lone transitive consumer of `zod@3.25.76` in `pnpm-lock.yaml`. Auditing proved `@tinybirdco/sdk` is completely unused across apps and packages (`packages/logger` uses raw `fetch()`). Dropping it eliminates Zod 3 entirely from the estate without code impact.
2. `GHSA-r4xh-jqrq-34v2` (smol-toml quadratic parse) is fixed upstream in `smol-toml >= 1.8.1` / `1.9.0`. Bumping the floor override in `deps-policy.json` and `w0m-web/package.json` to `>=1.9.0` eliminates the need to suppress it.
3. User requested all advisory suppressions, overridings, and disablings carry weighted justifications, risk boundaries, and tracking in `TECH-DEBT.md` (`D70`).

## Scope

- **In:**
  - Remove `@tinybirdco/sdk` from `w0m-web/package.json`.
  - Bump `smol-toml` to `>=1.9.0` in `project/policies/deps-policy.json` and `w0m-web/package.json`.
  - Re-run `pnpm install` in `w0m-web` to refresh `pnpm-lock.yaml`.
  - Delete `GHSA-mrhx-c7xc-m3w5` and `GHSA-r4xh-jqrq-34v2` from `w0m-web/osv-scanner.toml`.
  - Register `D70` in `TECH-DEBT.md` documenting the weighted risk matrix and perimeter isolation.
  - Verify `osv-scanner`, `pnpm why zod`, `pnpm why smol-toml`, and `just debt-check --enforce`.
- **Out:**
  - Upgrading offline build devDependencies (`glob 14`, `minimatch 11`) or breaking major framework changes (`astro 7.4+`).

- [x] **AC2:** Smol-toml patched: `pnpm why smol-toml` resolves to `>=1.9.0`, and `GHSA-r4xh-jqrq-34v2` is removed from `osv-scanner.toml`.
