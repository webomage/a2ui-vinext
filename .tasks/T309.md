# T309 — Align all just files across the estate to canonical 'justfile'

## Summary

`just` searches for command files using several fallback names (`justfile`, `Justfile`, `.justfile`). In practice, having mixed capitalization and hidden `.justfile` files across repositories creates inconsistency in CI, tooling, linters, and scripts (some scripts specifically look for `justfile` and miss `.justfile`, and macOS APFS case-insensitivity can obscure git tracking mismatches).

While the canonical `w0m7*` repositories have already standardized on lowercase `justfile`, other constituent repositories across the estate (notably in the `@k7` family like `k7d`, `k7llm`, `k7sol`, `k7spec`, `k7tg`, and personal toolsets) carried files named `Justfile` or hidden `.justfile`.

This ticket migrates all occurrences of `Justfile` and `.justfile` across self-owned/governed repositories to the uniform, canonical standard: **`justfile`**.

## Strict Boundary: Self/Governed Repos Only — Never Touch Client Repos

Client repositories (`_proj/*`, client delivery projects, third-party GH/Google accounts, completed or archived contracts) are strictly **NO-WRITE**. They may be completed, frozen under contract boundaries, or authenticated under foreign identities (enforcing the [[T265]] AC8 boundary). This migration strictly scopes to our self-owned and governed toolset repositories: `@self/@wom`, `@self/@k7`, and personal projects (`@self/@mob`, `@self/@value`).
