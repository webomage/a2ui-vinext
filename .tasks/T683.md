# T683 — Auto-Provision, Read, Update, and Guard All AI-DLC & SDLC Data Layers Across Worktrees and Session Wrap-Up

## Why

An end-to-end audit of `@wom`'s AI-DLC and SDLC data layers revealed 6 structural blind spots where data layers exist on disk in the canonical checkout or `~/.w0m/knowledge-index/corpus.sqlite` but were absent inside `agent-tree` worktrees, skipped during read queries (`just ask`, `just context-tree`), left `1..14` items stale on update (`session_complete.py`, `_post-deploy-knowledge`), or unguarded by `automation_health.py`:

1. **Worktree Graph Blindness (`scripts/release.py:provision_worktree_fixtures`):** `.codegraph/graph.db` and `graphify-out/graph.json` are gitignored across all member repos (`w0m-web`, `w0m-api`, `w0m-cf`, `w0m-skills`, `w0m-ext`, `w0m-sec`, `w0m-mlx/w0m-mlx`). Because `provision_worktree_fixtures()` did not copy them into `~/.w0m/work/<name>/w0m`, running `just ask`, `just graph-*`, or `just codemap-check` inside an isolated `agent-tree` worktree returned empty results or failed.
2. **Incomplete `just ask` & `.w0m/sdlc.json` Read Coverage:** `just ask` only looped over `root w0m-web w0m-api w0m-cf` (omitting `w0m-skills`, `w0m-ext`, `w0m-sec`) and never queried the Hybrid BM25 + 128-dim SVD Vector knowledge base (`scripts/w0m_kb_vector.py search` over `~/.w0m/knowledge-index/corpus.sqlite`).
3. **Harness² `context_tree.py` Rejects `DRAFT-<slug>` & Misses Multi-Layer Context:** `w0m-web/project/scripts/context_tree.py` rejected `DRAFT-<slug>` worktree ticket IDs and only materialized `task.md`, `diff.patch`, and `decisions/related.md` — omitting auto-queried `lessons/related.md` (`LESSONS.md`), `kb/related.md` (`w0m_kb_vector.py`), and `graph/symbols.md`, and was not auto-run by `ticket_new.py`.
4. **Stale Repo Graphs & Unsynced `codegraph` After Fast-Forward (`scripts/session_complete.py`):** `refresh_canonical_graph_union()` called `graph_global.py --auto-rebuild-stale` with the default `--stale-limit 10` (leaving repos `1..9` commits stale) and never ran `codegraph sync` on canonical repos after `git merge --ff-only origin/beta` (because `git merge --ff-only` does not trigger `post-commit` hooks).
5. **Unrefreshed Derived Surfaces & Post-GitHub-Promotion Mirror Lag (`scripts/session_complete.py`):** `harvest_and_refresh_surfaces()` omitted `codemap_gen.py --write` and `cp_feature_index.py`, and did not reconcile `w0m-web/project/tickets-sync-index.json` hashes after `sync_tickets_to_github()` wrote `gh_issue: <N>` and `tasks_render.py` updated `TASKS.md`.
6. **Unguarded Hybrid KB Vector Index (`scripts/w0m_kb_vector.py`, `scripts/automation_health.py`):** `~/.w0m/knowledge-index/corpus.sqlite` lagged by 14 tickets (`667` vs `681`) because neither `session_complete.py` nor `_post-deploy-knowledge` ran `w0m_kb_vector.py index`, and `automation_health.py` had no `bounded-kb-vector-lag` invariant.

## Scope

- **In:**
  - `scripts/release.py`: Copy `.codegraph/graph.db` and `graphify-out/{graph.json,GRAPH_REPORT.md}` from canonical estate repos into newly provisioned `agent-tree` worktrees in `provision_worktree_fixtures()`.
  - `justfile` & `.w0m/sdlc.json`: Expand `just ask` and `.w0m/sdlc.json -> codegraph.indexed` to all 6 `codegraph` repos (`w0m-web`, `w0m-api`, `w0m-cf`, `w0m-skills`, `w0m-ext`, `w0m-sec`), append top Hybrid BM25+Vector KB hits (`scripts/w0m_kb_vector.py search`) to `just ask`, and add `codemap-check`, `cp-feature-index-check`, and `decisions-check` to `session_wrapup.verification_gates`.
  - `w0m-web/project/scripts/context_tree.py` & `scripts/ticket_new.py`: Support `DRAFT-<slug>` ticket IDs in `context_tree.py`, materialize `lessons/related.md`, `kb/related.md`, and `graph/symbols.md` into `.w0m/context/<id>/`, and auto-invoke `context_tree.py` on `ticket_new.py` creation.
  - `scripts/session_complete.py` & `justfile` (`_post-deploy-knowledge`) & `just/estate.just` (`codegraph-sync`): Add `codemap_gen` and `cp_feature_index` plus `_refresh_synced_ticket_index_hashes` to `harvest_and_refresh_surfaces()`, re-run `harvest_and_refresh_surfaces()` after `sync_tickets_to_github()`, and upgrade `refresh_canonical_graph_union()` to run `codegraph sync` across canonical repos, `graph_global.py --auto-rebuild-stale --stale-limit 0`, and `w0m_kb_vector.py index`.
  - `scripts/automation_health.py`: Add `bounded-kb-vector-lag` check verifying `~/.w0m/knowledge-index/corpus.sqlite` ticket count lag `<= max_kb_vector_ticket_lag` (`25`).
  - `scripts/w0m_prs.py`: Register `casey/just` (`automation_mode: "banned"`, `casey/just#3227`) in `UPSTREAM_CONTRIBUTION_RULES`.
  - Unit tests in `scripts/tests/test_aidlc_data_layers.py`.
- **Out:**
  - Changing external MCP server schemas or introducing paid cloud vector databases (stays 100% local SQLite + FTS5 + SVD).
