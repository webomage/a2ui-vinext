# T439 — CP information architecture — one shell with a Me / Org / Admin context switcher, capability-grown menus, and DS layout components

## Summary

Owner, 2026-09-24: *"concentrate on reworking UI for CP and Job/Join — reorganise menu and pages in CP to make it
well structured for members and clients"*. Brainstormed and decided the same day. Sub-project **1 of 4**: this
ticket is the CP IA only.

**Measured today:**
- `apps/cp` serves 69 pages through two hand-built layouts. `AdminLayout.astro` (487 lines, 14 nav groups) serves
  `admin` and `member`; `member-routes.ts` filters members to 9 pages. `ClientLayout.astro` (310 lines) is a flat
  7-item `/client/*` list.
- [[T207]] (done 2026-09-14) made the nav *correct*: one source, capability-aware, every page placed. It grouped by
  URL domain (`/business`, `/system`, `/recruit`…), not by the tasks people come to do. Recruiting spans
  `/interviews`, `/recruit` and `/business/ats`, Integrations mixes system and marketing pages, and one person's
  roles are handled by a "hat" mode with an "acting under" banner.
- The schema already models the context this needs (AgDR-0019 / [[T117]]): `public.orgs` (`org_type` host / client /
  partner / vendor), `projects.host_org_id` + `projects.client_org_id`, and `hats.org_id` + `hats.office`.
- The design system has no shell or navigation components at all. `design-system/components` is Svelte-typed
  specs since [[T422]]; nothing React is left to port.
- The CP goldens cannot serve as a baseline. They show 9 nav groups against the live 14, `en-desktop-dashboard.png`
  is a captured 404 (`/ai/interviews`), and the Projects golden shows two List/Groups toggles and duplicate rows.

**Decided (owner, 2026-09-24):**
1. **Members = both.** The talent network (Join) and the delivery team are one member area that **grows by
   capability**, not separate apps.
2. **One shell with a context switcher.** The contexts are **Me** · **each org** you belong to · **Admin
   console**. Client and agency orgs use the same shell; `ClientLayout` and `/client/*` merge into it, and hats
   become "the role you hold in this context".
3. **Org first, project inside.** Projects are pages in an org, with breadcrumb `Org › Project` and project tabs.
   A delivered project appears in both its host and client org.
4. **Design the IA as design-system components**, mocked in the proposal with the real tokens, then implemented
   in `@webomage/ui`.

**Split out, as their own tickets (menu slots reserved here):** 2 — public personal and company profiles
(portfolio, confirmed tests, connected accounts, verification badges); 3 — brand/page claims and anti-squatting
(reserved slugs, proof of control, disputes); 4 — Job/Join UI rework on these patterns.
