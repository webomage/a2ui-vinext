# T195 — the red probes are not re-run, and the drift gate is drifting

## Summary

[[D37]] has carried `— (needs a T-row)` in its **Tracked** column since 2026-08-08. This is that
row, filed because the number it records **moved while nobody was watching**:

| | 2026-08-08 (D37, at [[T96]] AC5) | 2026-08-10 (measured) |
|---|---|---|
| undeclared `*-test`/`*-check` recipes | **13** | 🔴 **20** |

Seven new undeclared suites in two days. D37 said triaging the backlog was a prerequisite to
widening the matcher; the backlog grew 54% instead. Nothing reports this — `suites-drift` exits 1
and is wired into no hook, so its own drift is invisible by the same mechanism it exists to catch.

## 📍 The framing correction that makes this actionable

An external audit reported this on 2026-08-10 as *"20 quality and security checkers are currently
bypassed during automated test execution."* **That is wrong, and the wrong version is unfixable** —
it implies 20 missing gates. Classified:

| Kind | Count | What it actually is |
|---|---|---|
| **red probes** (`*-test`) | **18** | the tests that prove a gate *can go red* |
| **real gates** | **2** | `dep-licenses-check`, `cv-leak-check` |

Of the 2 real gates, **`dep-licenses-check` is wired into `.husky/pre-push`** and runs on every
push — not bypassed at all. That leaves exactly **one** genuinely ungated check:

🔴 **`cv-leak-check` (`justfile:1641`) is defined and wired NOWHERE** — not pre-commit, not
pre-push, not `just test`. A leak check that never runs.

⇒ **The real finding is not "20 checks are bypassed". It is: the estate has 18 red probes and
nothing re-runs them.** Every one of them exists to prove a gate is not vacuously green — the
estate's signature defect, the reason [[T135]] §4 and [[T160]] AC6 exist. A probe that is itself
never run is the same failure one level up: **we have proof the gates could fail, and no proof it
is still true.**

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] **AC1 — `cv-leak-check` gets a home or gets deleted.** It is the only genuinely ungated
      check of the 20. Wire it, or remove it with a reason — an unwired leak check is worse than
      no leak check, because its existence reads as coverage.
      ✅ **Wired into `.husky/pre-push`**, next to the licence gates — the same shape (a fail-closed
      gate paired with a probe that proves it goes red), which is the estate's own precedent. Green,
      0.6s measured. Also registered in the suite registry.
- [x] **AC2 — the 18 probes are registered**, in `project/test-suites.json`, in a tier that
      actually executes. ⚠️ **Measure the cost first and tier accordingly.**
      ✅ **Measured, and the worry was unfounded: all 27 are OFFLINE and cost 49.8s total.** Tiering
      is per-suite from measurement, not assumption — and two of the three tier signals I tried were
      wrong. Sniffing output for `infisical` mis-read `tools-check` as live (the word appears in the
      *tool inventory it prints*) and mis-read the four `authz-*-check` gates as offline (they use
      `infisical run --silent`, which suppresses the line). Reading the *recipe* was wrong too: none
      names infisical — the four authz gates reach the DB through `authz_probe` one level down.
      🔴 **THREE WERE RED AND NOBODY KNEW** — `client-inventory-test` 22/25, `client-profile-test`
      28/31, `skills-catalogue-test` 17/18. That is this ticket's thesis, arriving as evidence.
- [x] **AC3 — anything excluded carries a written reason**, which `run_suites.py` already refuses
      a registry without. "Slow" is a reason; silence is not.
      ✅ Three new exclusions, each with its reason: `graph-docs-cache-test` (a **timing demo** —
      `|| true` on both runs, no pass/fail, so registering it would add a suite that can never go
      red), `skills-sync` (a **writer**; `--check` only under `just skills-sync check`),
      `client-check` (needs a `<project-id>` argument — a per-project tool, not an unattended suite).
      The four red suites carry their failing assertion and promotion trigger.
- [x] **AC4 — widen the matcher (D37's actual subject).** `suites-drift` sees only names ending
      `-test`/`-check`, so `audit` and `py-typecheck` were **structurally invisible** to it.
      ✅ Widened from NAMES to BEHAVIOUR: a recipe is a suite if its name ends `-test`/`-check` **or**
      its body runs a `scripts/tests/` script or a `--check`/`--enforce`/`--drift` gate.
      🔴 **And that immediately exposed a second, larger hole nobody had named: 14 of the 15 newly
      found recipes end in `-test`/`-check` — they were inside the OLD matcher's own stated scope.**
      Its regex `^([a-z][a-z0-9-]*(?:-test|-check)):` required a bare `name:` and skipped **every
      recipe taking parameters** (`authz-blanket-check *args:`). ⇒ **D37's 13, its 20, and this
      ticket's 27 were all undercounts of the matcher's own definition** — a gate under-reporting
      within its own terms, which is the vacuous-green defect one level up. Registry: **70 declared,
      0 undeclared.**
- [x] **AC5 — `suites-drift` is itself wired.** It exits 1 today and blocks nothing, which is how
      13 became 20 unobserved. ⚠️ **re-read the trigger**, since T135's own history is of two gates
      that stayed `manual` for weeks after their triggers fired.
      ✅ **Wired into `.husky/pre-push` as BLOCKING, not `manual`** — I re-read the trigger as the AC
      instructs, and it had *already fired*: "promote when it exits 0" became true the moment the
      42 recipes were declared. Recording a promotion trigger for a condition already met is exactly
      the failure the warning describes.
- [x] **AC7 — `mmd-validate` is the blind spot made concrete; register it and give it a probe.**
      ✅ `just mmd-validate` + `just mmd-validate-test` (10 probes, `wom7skills/skills/mmd-validate/
      test.mjs`), both registered, and the pre-commit entry now goes through `just` per T130.
      The centrepiece probe is the 2026-08-10 regression itself: a **prose mention** of the
      ```` ```mermaid ```` fence must not open a phantom diagram — asserted by *count* (2 diagrams,
      not 3), since the original bug was a miscount rather than a crash. ⚠️ The probe **exits 2**
      when jsdom/mermaid are absent, because `validate.mjs` skips in that state and a green probe
      would be certifying a validator that validates nothing.
- [x] **AC6 — the red probe for the drift gate itself.** Add an undeclared recipe, observe
      `suites-drift` name it, remove it.
      ✅ Automated in `test_run_suites.py` (11 new probes) rather than done by hand: an undeclared
      recipe is **named**, the report **says why it matched**, and **declaring it clears the
      finding**. Includes the regression for today's discovery — parameterised and defaulted-param
      recipes — proven red against the old regex (`foo-check *args:` → MISSED,
      `bar-check mode=""`: → MISSED). Also guards over-matching: `:=` assignments, comments naming a
      `-check`, and ordinary recipes must NOT match, since a report full of non-suites is one people
      stop reading.
