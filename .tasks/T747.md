# T747 — Expand Core Services & Solutions matrix to 6 cards (3x2 / 2x3 / 1x6 across tablet and mobile orientations)

## Summary

Expand the **Core Services & Solutions** section (`HomeServices.astro` / `services.md`) on `https://webomage.com/services/` (and matching `apps/web` + `apps/cp` localized markdown & `knowledge-snapshot.json`) from 4 items (which leaves a lone 4th card on row 2 in a 3-column grid) to **6 balanced service cards** aligned with `PUBLIC_SERVICE_ACTION_CATALOG`, and lock the 4-posture responsive matrix (`AgDR-0054`) across desktop, tablet, and mobile orientations.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] AC1 — Author and validate OpenSpec change `openspec/changes/2026-11-15-services-core-matrix-6` defining the 6-item **Core Services & Solutions** matrix and the 4-posture responsive column counts (`3x2` desktop & tablet-landscape, `2x3` tablet-portrait & mobile-landscape, `1x6` mobile-portrait).
- [x] AC2 — Expand `services:` in `apps/web/src/content/pages/{,es/,ru/}services.md` and `apps/cp/src/content/pages/{,es/,ru/}services.md` (plus `apps/web/src/config/knowledge-snapshot.json` entry `174`) to **6 entries** covering all 6 core service pillars (`Compliance-as-Code & Audit Readiness`, `AI/LLM Adoption & Infrastructure`, `Enterprise DevOps & Platform Engineering`, `Surgical Tech Debt Elimination`, `Multi-Cloud Deployments & FinOps`, `Static Security & 12-Dimension UI/UX Audits`).
- [x] AC3 — Update `HomeServices.astro` (in `apps/web` and `apps/cp`) so the services matrix enforces balanced equal-height tracks (`auto-rows-fr items-stretch`) with responsive geometry: `1x6` on mobile portrait (`< 640px`), `2x3` on mobile landscape (`max-width: 900px` and `max-height: 600px` and `orientation: landscape`) and tablet portrait (`768px–1023px`), and `3x2` on tablet landscape (`1024px+` with height `> 600px`) and desktop (`1200px+`), backed by an automated Vitest suite + `astro check` + production build verification.
