## Summary

Recorded 2026-07-21, prompted by retiring the tech-debt DB in favour of plain markdown. **The
corpus already exists and is sizeable**: `openspec` 237 md · `docs/` 137 md · `project/tickets/`
71 md · plus `TASKS.md`, `ROADMAP.md`, `TECH-DEBT.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `docs/backend/KB-INDEX.md`. It
is git-tracked, diffable and human-readable — the question is how agents **find** the right slice
without reading everything.

**Evaluate:**
- **(a) plain `.md` + a good index/frontmatter convention** — cheapest; `KB-INDEX.md` is a partial start.
- **(b) Obsidian vault as local store** — three threads already exist and this should CONSOLIDATE
  rather than add a fourth: **T51** (vault ZIP import in CP Resource Library), **T24**
  (`kepano/obsidian-skills`), and `docs/GRAPHIFY.md §2` (graphify already writes node defs into a vault).
- **(c) embeddings/RAG over the corpus** — `documents` + `match_documents(p_agent_role)` already
  exist in the DB.
- **(d) do nothing new, lean on codegraph/graphify** (`docs/KNOWLEDGE-GRAPH.md`) — they answer
  *code* questions well but not *prose/decision* questions, which is the actual gap.
