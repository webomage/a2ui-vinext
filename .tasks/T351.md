# T351 — Mexican payment rails — Mercado Pago gateway, OXXO cash vouchers, and CLABE/SPEI bank transfers

## Summary

Mexican clients and domestic B2B engagements face high friction or total payment failure when international credit card processing (Stripe, [[T290]]) is the sole billing rail. SPEI interbank electronic transfers are the dominant B2B transaction mechanism across Mexico, and domestic cards/OXXO cash vouchers are standard for retail and smaller entities. This ticket introduces native Mexican payment rails to the client portal and invoice settlement engine: (1) Mercado Pago Checkout integration supporting Mexican credit/debit cards, OXXO cash vouchers, and Mercado Pago SPEI via hosted preferences, (2) Direct CLABE-based SPEI bank wire instructions per invoice, (3) Schema extension to `invoices.platform` supporting `stripe | mercado_pago | spei_direct` and local MXN currency accounting, and (4) Admin CP receipt reconciliation for manual SPEI wires.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC1 — Data model & schema extension: `invoices` supports `platform` values `'mercado_pago'` and `'spei_direct'` alongside `'stripe'`. Schema accommodates currency (`currency` defaulting to `'USD'` with support for `'MXN'`), exchange rate / local amount metadata, and unique SPEI transfer concept/reference tracking.
- [ ] AC2 — Mercado Pago preference creation backend: Edge function or CP API endpoint (`/api/payments/mercadopago-checkout` or `mercadopago-checkout`) creates a valid Mercado Pago Checkout preference for a client's invoice, handling amount conversion, line items, and callbacks (`success_url`, `failure_url`, `pending_url`).
- [ ] AC3 — Mercado Pago webhook handler: Webhook receiver (`/api/payments/mercadopago-webhook` or edge function `mercadopago-webhook`) validates IPN/webhook HMAC signatures, logs raw events into `public.platform_webhooks`, and marks the corresponding invoice as `paid` with `paid_at` timestamp upon verified payment.
- [ ] AC4 — Direct SPEI / CLABE wire instructions: Invoice model and rendering display standardized SPEI transfer coordinates (Beneficiary Name, Bank / STP, 18-digit CLABE, and uniquely generated invoice reference) for direct domestic wire transfer.
- [ ] AC5 — Client Portal invoice payment selector: `InvoiceList.svelte` provides a payment rail modal/picker allowing the client to choose between Stripe (International Card / USD), Mercado Pago (Mexican Card / OXXO / MP SPEI), and Direct SPEI Wire with one-click copyable CLABE details.
- [ ] AC6 — Admin reconciliation surface: Admin CP `/admin/finance` or `InvoiceManager` includes a manual confirmation action for finance operators to match bank deposits to open SPEI direct invoices, marking them paid and logging operator identity.
- [ ] AC7 — Test suite: Unit and integration tests covering MP preference creation, webhook signature verification/idempotence, CLABE validation, and invoice rail dispatch; workspace typecheck passes cleanly.
