# T351 — Mexican payment rails — Mercado Pago gateway, OXXO cash vouchers, and CLABE/SPEI bank transfers

## Summary

Mexican clients and domestic B2B engagements face high friction or total payment failure when international credit card processing (Stripe, [[T290]]) is the sole billing rail. SPEI interbank electronic transfers are the dominant B2B transaction mechanism across Mexico, and domestic cards/OXXO cash vouchers are standard for retail and smaller entities. This ticket introduces native Mexican payment rails to the client portal and invoice settlement engine: (1) Mercado Pago Checkout integration supporting Mexican credit/debit cards, OXXO cash vouchers, and Mercado Pago SPEI via hosted preferences, (2) Direct CLABE-based SPEI bank wire instructions per invoice, (3) Schema extension to `invoices.platform` supporting `stripe | mercado_pago | spei_direct` and local MXN currency accounting, and (4) Admin CP receipt reconciliation for manual SPEI wires.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] AC1 — Data model & schema extension: `invoices` supports `platform` values `'mercado_pago'` and `'spei_direct'` alongside `'stripe'` (`wom7api/supabase/migrations/20261021201000_t351_mexican_payment_rails_invoices.sql`). Schema accommodates currency (`currency` defaulting to `'USD'` with support for `'MXN'`), exchange rate / local amount metadata, and unique SPEI transfer concept/reference tracking.
- [x] AC2 — Mercado Pago preference creation backend: Edge function (`wom7api/supabase/functions/mercadopago-checkout/index.ts`) and CP API endpoint (`apps/cp/src/pages/api/payments/mercadopago-checkout.ts`) create a valid Mercado Pago Checkout Pro preference for a client's invoice, handling USD->MXN conversion, line items, and callbacks (`success_url`, `failure_url`, `pending_url`).
- [x] AC3 — Mercado Pago webhook handler: Webhook receiver (`wom7api/supabase/functions/mercadopago-webhook/index.ts` and `apps/cp/src/pages/api/payments/mercadopago-webhook.ts`) validates IPN/webhook HMAC-SHA256 signatures, logs raw events into `public.platform_webhooks`, and marks the corresponding invoice as `paid` with `paid_at` timestamp upon verified payment.
- [x] AC4 — Direct SPEI / CLABE wire instructions: `@webomage/lib/payments` (`mexican-rails.ts`) and `InvoiceList.svelte` display standardized SPEI transfer coordinates (Beneficiary Name, STP Bank 646, 18-digit Modulo-10 validated CLABE, and deterministic 7-digit Banxico SPEI reference) for direct domestic wire transfer.
- [x] AC5 — Client Portal invoice payment selector: `InvoiceList.svelte` provides a payment rail modal/picker (`data-testid="mexican-payment-rail-selector"`) allowing the client to choose between Stripe (International Card / USD), Mercado Pago (Mexican Card / OXXO / MP SPEI), and Direct SPEI Wire with one-click copyable CLABE & reference details.
- [x] AC6 — Admin reconciliation surface: `InvoiceManager.svelte` and `apps/cp/src/pages/api/admin/invoices/reconcile-spei.ts` include a manual "Reconcile SPEI" confirmation action for finance operators to match bank deposits to open SPEI direct invoices, marking them paid and logging `reconciled_by` and `reconciled_at`.
- [x] AC7 — Test suite: Unit and integration tests in `packages/lib/src/payments/mexican-rails.spec.ts` (8/8 passed) and `test_client_beta_epic.py` covering MP preference creation, webhook HMAC-SHA256 signature verification, ABM Modulo-10 CLABE validation, and invoice rail dispatch.
