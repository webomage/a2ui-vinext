## Summary

**Tours→docs/screencasts + publishable-docs program** — extend the T79 `TOURS` registry (`packages/ui/src/lib/components/wom/walkthrough/tours.ts`) into a generator that drives each tour with Playwright to bake (a) **screencasts** and (b) per-step **`.md`/`.pdf`** guides, in **member/client/trainee** variants, wired into the KB. + audit `docs/**` for **whitepaper/media/promo** publishability governed by [[kb-content-policy]] (client/project work never public) → publish-allowlist + public-docs pipeline.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC1 — **Tour-driven Playwright runner**: A generator or script consumes the `TOURS` registry from `packages/ui/src/lib/components/wom/walkthrough/tours.ts` and drives Playwright across each registered tour (`demo-home`, `client-portal`, `candidate-dashboard`, `web-home`, `job-board`) step by step against target DOM elements.
- [ ] AC2 — **Screencast and screenshot capture**: The runner records video/screencast clips (WebM/MP4) for each tour and captures per-step screenshot images highlighting targeted `[data-tour]` elements.
- [ ] AC3 — **Persona-scoped guide generation**: Generates structured `.md` guides and companion `.pdf` exports for each persona variant (`client`, `member`, `trainee`) containing ordered step titles, explanatory body text, and step visual captures.
- [ ] AC4 — **Knowledge Base integration**: Generated walkthrough guides are published into the KB documentation hierarchy (`docs/walkthroughs/` or `public.documents`) with frontmatter metadata tagging persona, route, and access scope.
- [ ] AC5 — **Publishable docs audit against [[kb-content-policy]]**: Complete an audit across `docs/**` cataloging documents for public/promo publication while strictly enforcing the [[kb-content-policy]] barrier: client engagements, project-specific deliverables, client PII, and rate data remain private/internal.
- [ ] AC6 — **Publish-allowlist and leakage guard**: An authoritative allowlist artifact defines approved public whitepapers and promo docs, with automated verification ensuring unlisted or non-conforming documents cannot be deployed or exposed to the public KB.

## Definition of Done

T97 is done when: a generator drives Playwright across every tour in the `TOURS`
registry (`demo-home`, `client-portal`, `candidate-dashboard`, `web-home`, `job-board`)
producing a screencast plus per-step screenshots for each, persona-scoped (`client`,
`member`, `trainee`) `.md`+`.pdf` guides are generated from those captures and published
into the KB with persona/route/access-scope frontmatter, a `docs/**` audit distinguishes
publishable from client/project-private content per [[kb-content-policy]], and an
allowlist artifact + automated guard prevents an unlisted or non-conforming document from
being deployed to the public KB.
