# T577 — Upwork Sidebar Category, Editable CV Title/Headline in Resume Studio & Work History Layout Fix

## Goal

1. Group `Contracts`, `Proposals`, and `Messages` under a dedicated `"Upwork"` parent category (`NavSection`) in the `job.webomage.com` (`apps/job`) `Me` sidebar, leaving `Connectors & sync` and `Outreach & clipper` under `Commercial & Sync`.
2. Add an editable **CV Title / Headline** input (`customRoleTitle`) in `ResumeStudio.svelte` and `@webomage/lib/resume` (`synthesis.ts`) so members can customize the role headline printed below their name on any CV variant or reset to the selected role persona's default title.
3. Fix `/me/work` (`WorkHistory.svelte`) where unlinked Upwork contracts were rendered inline as a multi-row wall of chips inside every CV experience row and stretched the left column vertically across the full height.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] **AC1 — Dedicated `"Upwork"` Sidebar Section (`apps/job/src/lib/context/nav.ts`)**:
  - Groups `Contracts` (`/me/contracts`), `Proposals` (`/me/proposals`), and `Messages` (`/me/messages`) under `{ label: "Upwork", items: [...] }` in `NAV.me` (`apps/job/src/lib/context/nav.ts`).
  - Keeps `Connectors & sync` (`/me/connectors`) and `Outreach & clipper` (`/me/outreach`) under `{ label: "Commercial & Sync", items: [...] }`.
- [x] **AC2 — Editable CV Title / Role Headline (`packages/lib/src/resume/synthesis.ts`, `ResumeStudio.svelte`)**:
  - Adds `customRoleTitle?: string` to `SynthesisOptions` in `packages/lib/src/resume/synthesis.ts`, overriding `roleDef.title` in `synthesizeResume()` and `renderResumeMarkdown()`.
  - Adds an always-visible `CV Title / Headline` input (`data-testid="cv-role-title-input"`) with a `Reset to role default` button right under **1. Select Target Role Persona** in `ResumeStudio.svelte`, and persists `custom_role_title` in saved variant `parsed_data`.
- [x] **AC3 — Compact Work History Contract-Linker Layout (`apps/cp/src/components/member/WorkHistory.svelte`)**:
  - Replaces the inline wall of 95 contract pills per CV position with a compact top-aligned row (`align-items: flex-start`, `align-content: flex-start`), relevance-sorted contract suggestions, a collapsible searchable contract picker drawer, and `@webomage/ui` `Button` / `Input` primitives.
  - Refreshes `docs/CP-FEATURE-INDEX.md` (`just cp-feature-index`) and verifies `just cp-nav-check` and unit tests (`synthesis.spec.ts`, `WorkHistory.test.ts`, `shared-sidebar.test.ts`).
