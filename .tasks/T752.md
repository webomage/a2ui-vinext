# T752 — Fix sidebar hat switcher double-scrollbar UX and isolate cp vs me sidebar links

## Summary

- Fixed `SidebarHatSwitcher.svelte` and `ContextSwitcher.svelte` scrollbar styling (`color-scheme: dark`, `scrollbar-width: thin`, `scrollbar-color: var(--border-default) transparent`, thin dark `::-webkit-scrollbar` rules, `overflow-x: hidden`), removed stacked double scrollports in favor of a single dark-styled floating/contained popover, formatted role rows vertically with single-line `truncate` on both title and `role_slug`, and added mutual exclusion via `wom:sidebar-switcher-opened` plus Escape/outside-click dismissal.
- Isolated `unifiedSwitcherEntries` and `unifiedSidebarSections` in `shared-sidebar.ts` (and `apps/me/src/lib/context/contexts.ts`) so `me.webomage.com` renders only `Me` (`/me`) and its same-origin `Talent Cloud` + `Me` sections with zero `cp.webomage.com` links, while `cp.webomage.com` renders same-origin `/me`, `/o/<slug>`, and `/admin` entries with CP's native `/me` sidebar sections and zero `me.webomage.com` links.

## Why

Sidebar role/hat switcher (SidebarHatSwitcher) showed bright-white native horizontal/vertical scrollbars, double cramped scrollports, long un-truncated role slugs, and simultaneous overlap with ContextSwitcher; plus shared-sidebar mixed cross-origin cp.webomage.com and me.webomage.com links causing full-portal jumps and sudden sidebar context loss.

## Scope

- **In:** `w0m-web/apps/cp/src/components/admin/SidebarHatSwitcher.svelte`, `w0m-web/packages/ui/src/lib/components/layout/ContextSwitcher.svelte`, `w0m-web/apps/cp/src/lib/context/shared-sidebar.ts`, `w0m-web/apps/me/src/lib/context/contexts.ts`, and their Vitest specs.
- **Out:** Database schema, auth/redirect gates (`apps/cp/src/lib/context/redirects.json`), and live portal deploy (awaiting owner deploy gate).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] **AC1:** Refine SidebarHatSwitcher and ContextSwitcher layout/CSS to eliminate white native scrollbars (color-scheme: dark + thin themed scrollbars), prevent horizontal overflow on long role slugs, unify/de-cramp project+role switching cleanly without native select elements, and mutually exclude open ContextSwitcher vs SidebarHatSwitcher menus.
- [x] **AC2:** Scope unifiedSwitcherEntries and unifiedSidebarSections cleanly per host portal (no cross-origin cp.webomage.com links mixed into me.webomage.com sidebar/switcher, and no me.webomage.com links mixed into cp.webomage.com sidebar/switcher) while keeping Me tools intact on me.webomage.com and CP context/org tools intact on cp.webomage.com.
- [x] **AC3:** Update shared-sidebar, ContextSwitcher, SidebarHatSwitcher, and CpShell tests, verify Vitest/svelte-check/astro-check, and pass session-complete.
