# T419 — Elevate Governance and Quality Gate Scripts to Workspace Root (Phase 3)

## Summary

Following the elevation of central registers and Tier 1 scripts, the remaining estate quality gates and policy checkers still execute from `wom7web/project/scripts/`.

This ticket elevates the governance and quality gate machinery to `@wom/scripts/` under **AgDR-0025** (zero fallback ladders, zero shadow symlinks, clean cutover):
1. Task & Ticket Integrity: `tasks_bounds.py`, `tasks_rotate.py`, `ticket_schema_check.py`, `ticket_deps.py`, `ticket_graph.py`, `ac_recipe_check.py`, `ac_trace_check.py`.
2. Spec & SDLC Quality Gates: `spec_drift_gate.py`, `spec_reality_check.py`, `spec_standard_check.py`, `spec_systems_gate.py`.
3. Estate Policies & Standards: `emoji_policy_check.py`, `doc_format_check.py`, `repo_hygiene.py`, `ide_trace.py`, `discovery.py`.
4. Tool & MCP Governance: `tools_check.py`, `tools_upgrade.py`, `mcp_declared.py`, `mcp_health.py`.
5. Estate Test Suites & Hook Runners: `run_suites.py`, `prepush_runner.py`.
