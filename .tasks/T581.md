# T581 — Job Portal Multi-Platform Profile Preparation Studio (Indeed, Proxify, Developers.net) & JOIN.com Job Boards/Services Catalog

## Context

To accelerate talent placement and outbound applications across global job boards and vetted engineering networks, the Job Portal (`apps/job`, `job.webomage.com`) needs:
1. **Multi-Platform Profile Preparation Tools** that transform a member's canonical career facts (`profile_cv_facts`, `profile_experience`, `SEEDED_CONTRIBUTION_DETAILS`, contact/accreditations) into tailored, ready-to-paste and ready-to-autofill profile packages for **Indeed** (`my.indeed.com/resume`), **Proxify** (`career.proxify.io`), **Developers.net / BairesDev** (`developers.net` / `applicants.bairesdev.com`), **Toptal**, **Turing**, **Gun.io**, **Lemon.io**, **X-Team**, **Contra**, **Wellfound**, and **JOIN.com Quick Apply**.
2. **JOIN.com Job Boards & Services Integration Catalog** enumerating all connectable job boards, vetted developer networks, freelance marketplaces, technical assessment gates (Codility, HackerRank, TestGorilla), and ATS/multiposting channels from `join.com` services listings alongside their integration mechanism (`Aggregator Feed`, `w0m-ext Form-Fill & Clipper`, `OAuth / MCP`, `JOIN.com Multipost`).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] **AC1 — Canonical JOIN.com Job Boards & Services Registry + Multi-Platform Profile Synthesizer (`@webomage/lib/resume`):** Create `w0m-web/packages/lib/src/resume/board-profiles.ts` (exported from `@webomage/lib/resume`) defining the 36-service `JOB_BOARD_SERVICES_CATALOG` across 4 tiers (`vetted-network`, `marketplace`, `join-multipost-board`, `ats-assessment`), `evaluatePlatformProfileReadiness`, and `synthesizePlatformProfile` for `indeed`, `proxify`, `developers-net`, `toptal`, `turing`, `gun-io`, `lemon-io`, `xteam`, `wellfound`, and `join-com`.
- [x] **AC2 — Declarative Extension Profile Form-Fill Specs (`w0m-ext`):** Add `w0m-ext/extension/specs/proxify.profile.json` and `w0m-ext/extension/specs/developersnet.profile.json` alongside `indeed.profile.json` for 1-click field population (`summary`, `stack_matrix`, `impact_story`, `availability`).
- [x] **AC3 — Job Portal Profile Preparation Studio & JOIN.com Services UI (`apps/job` & `apps/cp`):** Create `w0m-web/apps/cp/src/components/member/resume/PlatformProfileStudio.svelte` using `@webomage/ui` primitives and mount it in `w0m-web/apps/job/src/pages/me/resume.astro` and `w0m-web/apps/job/src/pages/me/connectors.astro` so members can inspect platform readiness scores (`0–100%`), copy/export tailored sections for Indeed, Proxify, Developers.net, etc., and browse/filter the 36-board JOIN.com & talent network catalog.
- [x] **AC4 — Verification & Unit Tests:** Add unit tests in `w0m-web/packages/lib/src/resume/__tests__/board-profiles.test.ts` verifying catalog integrity, readiness scoring, and profile synthesis for Indeed, Proxify, Developers.net, and JOIN.com.
