# T666 — Eliminate deploy-all browser-bundle node:* warnings, crawler-smoke 403, transcript noise, and Nov 20 Workers AI deprecated models

## Summary

Release `2026.11.07-1794034239` shipped cleanly to production (`deploy-logs/deploy-2026.11.07-1794034239.audit.json`) with `0` browser errors and `0` advisory failures, but surfaced three concrete deploy/smoke hygiene defects alongside Cloudflare's November 20, 2026 Workers AI model retirement notice (`https://developers.cloudflare.com/changelog/post/2026-11-05-planned-model-deprecations/`):

1. **6 Vite Browser-Bundle Externalization Warnings (`apps/cp` + `apps/me`)**:
   - `w0m-web/packages/lib/src/connectors/linkedin-manual-intake.ts` imports `node:fs` and `node:path` solely for `verifyNoLinkedInAutomationInExt()` (called only by `linkedin-manual-intake.test.ts`), yet is re-exported by `@webomage/lib/connectors` into client Svelte islands (`ConnectionsManager.svelte`, `OrgIntegrationsManager.svelte`, `ConnectedIdentities.svelte`), producing 4 `[WARN] [vite] [plugin rolldown:vite-resolve]` warnings (`2` in `cp`, `2` in `me`).
   - `w0m-web/packages/kyc/src/biometric.ts` imports `createHash` and `createHmac` from `node:crypto` for `computeBiometricBindingDigest()`, which is re-exported by `@wom/kyc` into `ProfessionalSelfAccounting.svelte` (`cp` + `me`), producing 2 `[WARN] [vite]` warnings.
2. **`crawler-smoke` `HTTP 403` (`error code: 1010`) in `just smoke-live`**:
   - `scripts/live_verify_helper.py` (`run_crawler_smoke`) posts to `https://webnames-crawler.webomage.workers.dev/enqueue` via `urllib.request.Request` without a `User-Agent`, sending `Python-urllib/3.x` which Cloudflare edge Browser Integrity Check rejects with `HTTP 403 error code: 1010`.
3. **Deploy Transcript Noise & False-Positive `other_trouble` Classifications**:
   - `check-auth` in `justfile` runs `supabase projects list` without a linked `.temp/project-ref`, printing `Cannot find project ref. Have you run supabase link?` on every `deploy-api` run.
   - `w0m-web/.npmrc` holds pnpm-only keys (`enable-pre-post-scripts=true`, `verify-deps-before-run=true`) that cause Gauge's JS launcher (`npm`) during `smoke-live` / `goldens` to emit `npm warn Unknown project/env config`.
   - `justfile` `deploy-functions` has an un-`@`-prefixed `# Bulk deploy, then VERIFY each function updated and retry...` comment echoed to stderr, and `scripts/deploy_logged.py` (`audit_transcript`) flags `Uploading asset (upwork-poller): ...upwork-failure.ts` and headless `[Browser WARNING]: No available adapters.` under `other_trouble`.
4. **Cloudflare November 20, 2026 Workers AI Model Deprecations (24 models -> `HTTP 410` / `5028`)**:
   - While all 22 active rows in `public.ai_models` are already clean, `@cf/google/embeddinggemma-300m` remains in `ALLOWED_EMBEDDING_MODELS` (`w0m-api/src/index.ts` and `w0m-web/project/scripts/ai_models_check.py`), `@cf/qwen/qwen3-30b-a3b-fp8` remains in `w0m-api/scripts/resume-assist-eval.ts`, and `RETIRED_MODELS` in `ai_models_check.py` only guards 4 legacy strings instead of all 24 retiring IDs.
