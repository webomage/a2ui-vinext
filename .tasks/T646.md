# T646 — Unify `job` & `join` portals + personal `/me/*` + public `/u/*` into `me.webomage.com` (`apps/me`) and realign `cp.webomage.com` strictly around org, project, crew & admin

## Summary

Unify `w0m-web/apps/job` (`job.webomage.com`) and `w0m-web/apps/join` (`join.webomage.com`) together with universal personal `/me/*` tools and public personal profiles (`/u/[slug]/*`) into a single Personal & Talent Cloud portal **`me.webomage.com` (`w0m-web/apps/me`, Worker `webomage-me`)**, while realigning **`cp.webomage.com` / `cp.w0m.xyz` (`w0m-web/apps/cp`, Worker `webomage-cp`)** strictly around Organization (`/o/[org]/*`), Project (`/o/[org]/p/[project]/*`), Crew Delivery (`/me/work`), and Platform Admin (`/admin/*`) per `AgDR-0056` and `AgDR-0025` (zero backward-compatibility shims, clean `301` redirects on retired routes/hosts, and atomic updates across all estate repos).

Consolidating `apps/job` + `apps/join` into `apps/me` reduces Cloudflare SSR Workers from `5` to `4` (`AgDR-0055` Free-Tier-First), repatriates all org-scoped commercial & recruiting routes (`/o/[org]/money/{contracts,proposals}/*`, `/o/[org]/outreach`, `/o/[org]/people/recruiting/*`) back into `apps/cp` so 100% of org workflows live under one roof, establishes `https://me.webomage.com/u/<slug>` as the canonical public profile URL (`301`-redirecting `webomage.com/u/*` and `w0m.xyz/u/*`), and fixes two live `apps/join` defects (`adminClient()` + `system_prompt` serialization in `/interview/[id]`, and unauthenticated RLS fallback to `demoCandidateFixture` in `/dashboard`).
