# T77 — Reconcile the openspec spec-drift backlog

## Summary

The T23 **spec-drift-gate** (`project/scripts/spec_drift_gate.py`, `just spec-drift`) surfaced a
pre-existing backlog of drift between `openspec/changes/` and the tickets/code. The gate blocks

the audit runs clean. Baseline (2026-07-17): **16 active changes**, 1 hard-drift + 10 orphans.
