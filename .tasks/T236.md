## Evidence — 2026-08-20

**AC1 — deleted, after re-measuring as the AC demanded.** That insistence earned its keep: the
re-measure showed **1 reference each in `apps/web`** that the original count missed. It turned out to
be `apps/web/.astro/dev.log` — a build log from that evening's dev server, gitignored. **A generated
artefact mentioning a component is not a reference**, and a less careful pass would have either
aborted the deletion or ignored a real hit. Final state before removal: `TemplateManager` identical
to cp's (0 differing lines), `CandidateManager` differing by 14 with **cp strictly newer**.

**AC2 — swept, and the census was indeed a lower bound.** 100 `.svelte` components across 6 apps;
**7 are referenced by nothing in their own app**:

| app | component |
|---|---|
| cp | `MilestoneBadge` · `SvelteCounter` · `admin/HatchetActivity` · `dashboard/CommitGraph` |
| web | `MilestoneBadge` · `SvelteCounter` |
| join | `interviews/Card` |

⚠️ **Deliberately NOT deleted.** The two removed under AC1 had three independent lines of evidence —
git history, a one-directional divergence showing cp strictly newer, and the 404 route analysis.
These seven have **a grep**. Spot-checked three: zero mentions anywhere in `.astro`/`.svelte`/`.ts`/
`.json`/`.md`, and the estate loads **no** component dynamically (no `import(\`…\`)`, no
`components/${…}`), so the method is sound — but sound enough to *report* is not sound enough to
*delete seven files on*. They want a decision, not a sweep. 📌 Note `MilestoneBadge` and
`SvelteCounter` are orphaned in **both** cp and web — duplicated and dead in two places.

**AC4 — `just orphan-components`, red-proven three ways:** removing `BlueprintManager`'s only mount
makes it appear and `--enforce` exit **1**; restoring the mount clears it; a broken matcher exits
**2 — unknown, never a clean 0**. It looks at MOUNTING rather than data access, because the RLS
analysis that found the original two would never have surfaced a component that queries nothing.
