# T672 — Persist order form & filter state across page refreshes, add Job Board recency filters (`day`/`week`/`month`), and surface submitted bug reports

## Summary

Addresses three live operator and user experience gaps across `webomage.com`, `me.webomage.com`, and `cp.webomage.com`:
1. **Order Form & Scope Calculator State Persistence Across Page Refresh**: Refreshing the browser page (`F5` / `Cmd+R`) on `/blueprints/order` (`BlueprintOrderFlow.svelte`), `/audit/` (`SecurityAuditWidget.svelte`, `AuditPricing.svelte`), `/services/` (`ComplianceAuditCatalogExplorer.svelte`), or Client Portal (`ClientDashboard.svelte`, `SecurityAuditReportViewer.svelte`) previously reset in-progress inputs, step progression, and scope selections.
2. **Job Board Time Filters (`day`, `week`, `month`, `quarter`, `all`) on `me.webomage.com`**: The aggregated remote job board (`JobBoard.svelte` + `w0m-api/supabase/functions/jobs-list/index.ts`) lacked recency filters (`Past 24h`, `Past Week`, `Past Month`, `Past 3 Months`, `All Time`) — directly triggering the live bug report filed from `https://me.webomage.com/` (`8090dd5b-59ab-47d8-9c75-192bf0fab5f3`: `"we have to have better filters here"`).
3. **Submitted Bug Report Visibility**: `BugReportWidget.svelte` showed only a static `"Report Submitted!"` confirmation with no way to view previously submitted bug reports, and `GET /api/bug-report?projectId=...` excluded platform-wide widget bug reports (`project_id IS NULL`) such as `"we have to have better filters here"` (`me.webomage.com`) and `"/me/profile/resume"` (`cp.webomage.com`).

Additionally, the 33 newly forked `casualuser/*` and `webomage/*` repositories (`memorilla`, `laya-mlx`, `grim-prompter`, `MinerU`, `auto-ontology`, `openlit`, `aidlc-workflows`, `SPACE`, `AnyJev`, `SkillSpector`, etc.) have been cataloged into a dedicated backlog ticket [[T673]] and repo-first research report [`.agents/reports/2026-11-08-casualuser-forked-tools-integration-backlog.md`](../.agents/reports/2026-11-08-casualuser-forked-tools-integration-backlog.md).
