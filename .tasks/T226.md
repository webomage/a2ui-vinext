# T226 — `client-inventory` and `skills-catalogue` are red about client repos

> Found 2026-08-16 diagnosing the four advisory failures the owner spotted in the deploy log.
> [[T224]] fixed the blocking suites; these two were never diagnosed.

## Summary

`just test` reports **4 advisory suites failing**. Two are now fixed ([[T224]]: `deploy_logs` and
`ide_surfaces` were in no profile tier, which broke `client-profile-check` **and**
`client-profile-test`). The remaining two assert facts about **client** repos:

| Suite | Failing assertions |
|---|---|
| `client-inventory-test` | *"artemio's 33 runtime skills are governed — manifest + lock, 23 declared"* · *"artemio now matches the estate layout — 23 governed, 23 symlinks + 10 real dirs"* · *"no project still carries a retired `.agent/skills` tree"* · *"shared client gaps are down to the two keys the profile deliberately excludes"* |
| `skills-catalogue-test` | 15/18 — *"exactly 2 skills ship runnable files — measured, and small"* · *"every skill shipping runnable files has an EXPLICIT decision with a reason"* · *"host invocation is MEASURED — artemio's idea-refine is seen running at justfile:215"* |

Every one hard-codes a **count or a line number** measured against a client repo at a moment in
time (23 declared, 33 runtime, 2 runnable, `justfile:215`). Those repos have moved.

⚠️ **Do not assume these are stale assertions.** The alternative is that a client genuinely
regressed — an ungoverned skill tree or a retired `.agent/skills` reappearing is exactly what these
were written to catch. **Which it is, is unestablished**, and that is AC1.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] **AC1 — For each failing assertion, say whether the CLIENT changed or the TEST rotted.**
      With evidence from the client repo, not inference. These have opposite fixes.
- [x] **AC2 — Kill the hard-coded coordinates.** An assertion pinned to `justfile:215` or "exactly
      2" fails on any unrelated edit. Assert the property (*a skill shipping runnable files has a
      recorded decision*), not the census.
- [x] **AC3 — Decide advisory vs blocking, explicitly.** They are advisory today with a reason; if
      they can only ever be advisory because they measure repos outside this estate's control, say
      so. A permanently-red advisory suite trains people to skim past the whole block.
- [x] **AC4 — Prove each fixed suite goes red on a real regression**, not just green today.
