# T259 — a deploy that records nothing

## Summary

Found by [[T258]] while restoring the changelog. The newest record in **both** `public.deploy_log`
and `wom7api/deploy-logs/` is **2026-08-16**. But two deploys happened on **2026-08-22**: the
`agent-consultant` edge function and the worker, both confirmed by the owner in-session
(*"deployed"*, *"consultant deployed"*).

**Neither appears anywhere.** Not in the table, not in the transcripts, not in the changelog.

## Why

`deploy-all` self-wraps through `deploy_logged.py` (fixed 2026-08-16, after being opt-in and
therefore unused). A **direct** `supabase functions deploy` or `wrangler deploy` does not — and
those are the paths a targeted single-service deploy actually takes. So the recording surface covers
the *aggregate* recipe and not the *narrow* ones, which are the common case when shipping one fix.

🔴 **The failure mode is silence.** Nothing errors, nothing warns; the record simply has a hole, and
the hole looks exactly like a period with no deploys. [[T258]]'s `just changelog-drift` cannot see
this either — it compares the changelog against the transcripts, and an unrecorded deploy is missing
from **both** sides, so the comparison agrees. ⚠️ **A gate whose two inputs share the same blind spot
reports green about the thing it cannot see.**

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **AC1** — enumerate every path that reaches production: the `deploy*` recipes, direct
      `supabase functions deploy`, `wrangler deploy`, `cf-apply`, and any dashboard action. Measured
      from the justfile and the hooks, not recalled.
- [ ] **AC2** — every enumerated path either routes through `deploy_logged.py` or is declared
      **unrecordable with a reason** (a dashboard click cannot be wrapped) — printed on every run,
      so the residue stays visible rather than becoming an assumption.
- [ ] **AC3** — a gate that can detect an **unrecorded** deploy, which requires a source outside the
      two that already share the blind spot: the live `/health` version endpoint (ROADMAP §4 already
      stamps it) compared against the newest `deploy_log` stamp. 🔑 If `/health` reports a version
      the table has never seen, a deploy happened that nothing recorded.
- [ ] **AC4** — red first, on the two deploys from 2026-08-22 if they are still detectable at
      `/health`, or on a synthetic version otherwise.
- [ ] **AC5** — registered in `project/test-suites.json`; `tier: live` with a **written reason** for
      its blocking status, since `run_suites.py:80` rejects a reasonless non-blocking suite.
