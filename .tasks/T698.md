# T698 — Public Service & DevOps Action Ordering Configurator + Parallel Agent Ticket Collision Guards

## Summary

Restore and upgrade interactive public service ordering forms on `/solutions` (`SolutionsPage.astro`, `SolutionsGrid.astro`) and `/services` (`ServicesPage.astro`, `ServicesDetailed.astro`) via `ServiceActionOrderConfigurator.svelte` (`ServiceOrderSection.astro`) and `order-form-state.ts`, enabling visitors to order every core Webomage service (Multi-Cloud Deployments & Migration, Enterprise DevOps & Platform Reliability, Compliance-as-Code & Data Recovery, Enterprise AI Chatbots & RAG, Surgical Tech Debt Recovery, and Read-Only Skill/Repo/UI-UX Craftsmanship Audits) either as a **Fixed-Scope Package** (Registration + Free Baseline Audit in-between + Stripe Checkout) or **By Request / Custom Scoped Action** (Registration + Free Baseline Audit + AI Agent Helper handoff) with rich multi-select chips for **Source Infrastructure**, **Target Infrastructure**, **Involved Workloads & DevOps Actions**, and **Cutover Timeline**. In addition, harden `scripts/ticket_new.py` and `scripts/session_complete.py` against parallel-agent ticket-number collisions and post-commit gate rollback leaks.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] AC1 — **Structured Service, Infrastructure Selector & Fast OAuth Catalog (`w0m-web/packages/lib/src/security/order-form-state.ts`):** Defined `PUBLIC_SERVICE_ACTION_CATALOG` (6 core services matching `/solutions` and `/services` with package pricing, `targetKind`, and default selectors), `SERVICE_SOURCE_INFRA_OPTIONS`, `SERVICE_TARGET_INFRA_OPTIONS`, `SERVICE_WORKLOAD_SCOPE_OPTIONS`, `SERVICE_CUTOVER_TIMELINE_OPTIONS`, `SUPPORTED_FAST_AUTH_PROVIDERS` (`google`, `github`, `linkedin_oidc`), `buildFastOAuthRegisterUrl()`, `ORDER_FORM_STORAGE_KEYS.serviceActionOrder`, `resolvePublicServiceAction()`, and `formatServiceOrderContextSummary()`.
- [x] AC2 — **Optional-by-Default Infra/URL Fields & 1-Click OAuth Registration (`ServiceActionOrderConfigurator.svelte`, `SecurityAuditWidget.svelte`, `BlueprintOrderFlow.svelte`, `AuditPricing.svelte`, `ServiceOrderSection.astro`, `sectionRegistry.ts`, `security-audit.ts`):** Built `ServiceActionOrderConfigurator.svelte` and updated all public ordering/registration forms so target URLs and infrastructure selectors are optional by default (`Skip Details & Fast Register` / `Skip URL & Fast Register`) and visitors can register in 1 click via Google, GitHub, or LinkedIn OAuth (`https://cp.webomage.com/api/auth/oauth?provider=google|github|linkedin_oidc&next=...`) or via passwordless Work Email Magic Link.
- [x] AC3 — **Direct Card-to-Configurator Wiring on `/solutions` and `/services` (`SolutionsPage.astro`, `SolutionsGrid.astro`, `ServicesPage.astro`, `ServicesDetailed.astro`):** Rendered `ServiceOrderSection` on `/solutions` and `/services` and wired solution cards and service track/stage cards via `wom:select-service-order` to pre-select the corresponding service slug/mode and smooth-scroll directly to the infrastructure target selectors (`#service-order-configurator`).
- [x] AC4 — **Parallel Agent Session & Ticket-Numbering Collision Guards (`scripts/ticket_new.py`, `scripts/session_complete.py`):** Updated `scripts/ticket_new.py` (`allocate_ticket`) to disambiguate multiple draft tickets in the same `agent/<slug>` worktree without overwriting `DRAFT-<slug>.md`, and updated `scripts/session_complete.py` (`_soft_reset_worktree_to_merge_base`, `_rollback_renumbered_tickets`, `sync_worktree_onto_origin_beta`) to roll back `T<N> -> DRAFT-<slug>` even when `commit_dirty_repos` already created a local commit before a certification failure and to preserve branch-added `LESSONS.md` lines across worktree syncs.
