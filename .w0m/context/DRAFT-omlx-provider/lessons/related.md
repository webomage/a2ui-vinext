  CONTEXT: **2026-09-18 (T153 / Local Gemma 4 on Apple Silicon oMLX).** Running multi-model local inference on a 32 GB unified memory Mac without strict allocation ceilings allowed oMLX 0.5.3 to balloon to 22 GB resident RAM + 12 GB compressed memory.
